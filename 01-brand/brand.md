# Brand

## Who we are
- Achieve Sports Performance is an S&C facility at 4020 Fox Valley Center Drive, Aurora, IL (outside Fox Valley Mall), open since 2016. (Atlas 2026-09-01; Maya 2026-08-21)
- Swim dryland is the zone. Also athletic development, adult semi-private personal training (4:1), remote programming, and sports massage.
- Athletes start at age 11. The face of the brand is varsity and serious high school or competitive athletes, not 11 to 15 year olds (we still train them).

## Promise
- "Better Performance in Your Sport with Fewer Injuries."
- Completely individualized: an evaluation on force plates (Hawkin), then a program written from that athlete's jumps, sport, position, and history. Not generic group workouts.

## Proof (use only on varsity or commit surfaces, not morning-adult ads)
- About 10-year track record: about 130 college commits (about 70% D1), 6 Olympic trials qualifiers, many state champions. (Atlas 2026-09-01; Maya 2026-09-10)
- Never invent a school, sport, time, or name when writing a commit post.

## Voice
- Coach as expert, athlete as proof. Educational, not hype.
- Social captions: fuller plain coach prose, not choppy fragments. (Maya 2026-09-07)
- Landing and ad copy: fuller and premium; headlines make a parent want to train here and request the eval. Do not H1 that the program is written only from jumps. (Maya 2026-09-01)
- Banned words: "elite", "high-level". Banned: "complimentary", "1:1 eval" (say "evaluation" or "20-minute screen").

## Look
- Live site palette: black, charcoal, white, deep red. Tokens #c82536, #7e1720, #434549, #161616, #000000, #ffffff, off-white #ededed. Not navy. Nav font Metropolis Bold. (Atlas 2026-09-01)
- Before any ad, graphic, or landing page, pull hex, type, and photos from the live site (achievesportsperformance.com).

## Social
- Instagram: @achieve_sportsperformance. Default post is a named varsity athlete plus one real number (jump, time, or commit), or a coach-cued drill that looks like high school work. (Atlas, Maya 2026-08-25)
- Do not lead the grid with morning adults or body-comp content; those stay in Meta ads or labeled Stories.
- Overachiever spotlight posts: 40 to 90 words, warm, one concrete proof point, exactly 5 hashtags. (Maya 2026-08-24)
- Business X/IG drafts: Nova. Ryne's personal X/IG is Echo (personal-brain), not this brand.
- Related skills: `achieve-dryland-captions`, `achieve-writing-style`.
