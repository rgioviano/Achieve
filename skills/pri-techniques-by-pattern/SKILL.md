# PRI techniques by pattern and position

Full step scripts live in the source file this skill was built from. Open the pattern section, then the earliest position.

Pattern pick: Patho PEC if they can toe-touch or deep-squat and bilateral ADT and PADT are positive. PEC if bilateral extension and they cannot toe-touch (opening drills stay in the PEC skill). Left AIC uses supine then sidelying, and the non-responder block here only if 90-90 does not change them. Right BC subscap sheets only after a left zone of apposition, and only if apical expansion is not still asymmetric (left low trap and serratus first when it is).

Core opening scripts (90-90 hemibridge, 90-90 balloon, right sidelying left adductor, left sidelying right glute max, hemi 90-90, standing wall reach, modified all-four belly lift, wall squat with balloon) stay in Left AIC, PEC, Patho PEC, and Right BC.

Injury gates, not new protocols: shoulder impingement or instability keeps left IO/TA and uses subscapularis not lat, and only after the thorax gate. Hip pain on a patho screen starts in Patho PEC, not glute med. Hip OA can muddy ADT, PART, and PADT. SI pain still uses the toe-touch gate. Low back has no dedicated ladder. Do not invent one. Neck and thoracic outlet manual branches stay out of the gym. Knee and ankle are not their own menu. Swim and overhead IR is subscap after the thorax is neutral.

Squatting Bar Reverse Reach is one sheet (goal is PRI squat level four or five). There is no separate level 2 and level 3 setup. Do not swap in the Fitness and Movement bar reach. Three Point Stance does not name a side.

The numbered steps below are the 2012 technique sheets, wording kept.

## Left AIC non-responder order
All four posterior mediastinum, then wall squat with right apical expansion, then short-seated left arm reach (balloon, then no balloon), then kneeling bar reach, then standing resisted alternating reach, then forward walking reach, then late left stance with right arm reach. Not the first block of a normal Left AIC session.

### All four: All Four Left Posterior Mediastinum Expansion in Left AF IR
1. Hands and knees, 1-inch towel under the left knee.
2. Round the spine and roll the pelvis so the bottom tucks.
3. Inhale through the nose and gently press down with both arms, filling the left upper back.
4. Exhale and hold.
5. 4-5 breaths, fill the left upper back on each inhale.
6. Relax and repeat 4 more times.

### Standing squat: Wall Supported Squat with Respiratory Right Apical Expansion
Heels 7-10 inches from the wall. 4-6 inch ball between the knees. Bottom on the wall, slight knee bend, pelvic tilt, low back flat. Squeeze the ball, inhale. Exhale and reach both arms forward. Then exhale and reach only the right arm, inhale into the back of the chest, exhale and reach the right arm further, one more breath. Then inhale and reach the left arm, exhale and hold. Inhale and reach the left arm further and slightly right so the nose is over the right knee, weight on the left, left outside hip on. Repeat that once. Stand up still shifted left. Relax and repeat the whole sequence 4 more times. Reference: left abs, left heel, right arch.

### Short seated: Wall Short Seated Left Arm Reach with Balloon
1. Sit, mid to low back flat on the wall, legs straight.
2. Balloon in the right hand.
3. Knees to the chest, one leg at a time.
4. Inhale and reach the left hand forward. Right mid back stays on the wall. Left mid back may come forward.
5. Exhale into the balloon.
6. Pause 3 seconds, tongue on the roof of the mouth.
7. Do not pinch the balloon. Inhale and reach the left hand further, filling the back of the chest.
8. Exhale into the balloon again.
9. 4-5 breaths, further left reach on each inhale.
10. Relax and repeat 4 more times.
Reference: left abs, left sit bone, left heel, right arch.

### Short seated: Wall Short Seated Left Arm Reach (no balloon)
Same wall sit. Knees to the chest, knees together, feet slightly apart, no outward rotation. Inhale. Exhale and reach both arms, back on the wall from bra-line to belt-line, pause 3 seconds. Then inhale and reach only the left hand, right mid back flat, left mid back may come forward. Exhale and hold 3 seconds. Further left reach on the next inhale. 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Kneeling: Kneeling Bar Reach
1. On the knees, bar in front, pillows under the bottom if needed.
2. Roll the pelvis back, back rounds.
3. Hold 4-5 breaths, fill the upper back on the inhale.
4. Relax and repeat 4 more times.

### Standing: Standing Resisted Alternating Respiratory Reach
Tubing anchored behind, both hands, arms at shoulder level. Left hip back, weight on the left midfoot and heel, left outside hip on. Inhale and reach the left arm, fill the left upper back. Exhale and reach the right arm. Repeat 4-5 breaths. Then the same with the right hip back and the right arch down. Repeat 4 more times.

### Standing: Forward Alternating Reciprocal Resisted Respiratory Reach
Tubing in each hand, resistance behind. Look straight ahead. Left hip back, inhale, left arm and right leg forward, fill the left upper back. Exhale, right foot down, right hip back. Inhale, right arm and left leg forward, fill the right upper back. Exhale, left foot down. Walk 10 steps. Repeat 2 more times.

### Standing: Late Left AIC Stance with Right Arm Reach
Left foot on a 1-inch block, right leg behind. Round the back, look ahead. Right hip forward or left hip back, weight on the left midfoot and heel, right heel down, left knee slightly bent, chin and zipper over the left foot. Left thigh and left outside hip on. Right arm up, left arm down, sidebend left, left abs on, stretch in the right chest. Inhale and exhale while reaching the right arm forward and up, 4-5 breaths, fill the right chest on the inhale. Repeat 4 more times.

## Patho PEC step 2
After 90-90 Hip Lift in Passive FA IR with Balloon. Standing, then all four, then seated. Before Left AIC facilitation.

### Standing: Wall Supported Passive FA IR with Balloon
Ball between the knees, feet 7-10 inches from the wall and wider than the hips. Pelvic tilt, low back flat, upper back off the wall. Inhale, blow into the balloon, pause 3 seconds, tongue up. Inhale again without pinching, upper back expands. Blow again. No neck or cheek strain. After the fourth inhale, pinch the balloon and let the air out. On the last exhale, stand through the heels with the low back flat. Repeat 4 more times.

### Standing: Wall and Chair Supported IO/TA
Same wall and ball, hands on a chair, look ahead. Low back and mid back flat, upper back off. Inhale. Exhale and press the hands into the chair. Front of thighs and outer abs on. Inhale and relax the hands but keep the abs on. Upper back expands. 4-5 breaths. Stand through the heels on the last exhale. Repeat 4 more times.

### Standing squat: Squatting Bar Reverse Reach
Wooden pole across a door frame. Best squat with heels down and knees inside the elbows, bottom of the pelvis toward the heel cords. Inhale through the nose into the back of the chest, eyes level. Exhale and lean back until the air is out. 3 to 4 more breaths. Heel cords, front of thighs, and back muscles stay soft. After the fourth inhale, exhale and stand, pushing through the heels, back rounded, pole slides up the frame. Repeat 4 more times. Goal is the pole at the ankle, elbows straight, knees in the chest, pelvis on the heel cords (PRI squat level four or five). Once that is honest, after the fourth inhale reach forward on the exhale so the pole leaves the frame, and stand while still reaching. A 1 inch by 4 foot pole, pipe insulation for cushion.

### All four: All Four Belly Lift (non-modified)
Hands and knees, back rounded. Knees off the mat so they are straight. Weight forward, nose over the fingertips, outer abs on. Shift left and lift the right hand slightly, left abs on, 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times. The sheet also allows the other side: shift right and lift the left hand.

### Seated: Seated Bar Reach
Chair, feet flat, knees at or slightly above the hips. Bar on the outside of a door frame, hands slightly below shoulder level. Inhale, exhale, round the back, take up slack, pelvis rolled back. Stretch across the back. Hold 4-5 breaths, fill the back of the chest. Repeat 4 more times.

## Right BC after the thorax gate
Position order when the cluster is clear: supine, sidelying, seated, prone, standing. Do not start these while apical expansion is still asymmetric. Three Point Stance is unsided. The QL stretch is not a low-back protocol.

### Supine: Supine Resisted Right HG IR
Back, knees bent, arms at shoulder level on bolsters, elbows 90. Tubing from above the right hand into both hands. Left thumb toward the body. Right palm toward the body. Shoulder blades down and together. Straighten the right elbow against the tubing. Then turn the right hand down toward the mat. Front of the right shoulder on. Hold 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Supine: Supine Resisted Right HG IR with Weighted Left HG ER
Tubing above the right arm, weight in the left hand, arms on bolsters, elbows 90. Left hand on the mat, right hand toward the ceiling. Blades down, elbows into the bolsters. Turn the right hand down and lift the left hand until they match. Front of right shoulder and back of left shoulder. Hold 4-5 breaths. Lower back to start. Repeat 4 more times.

### Supine: Supine Resisted Right Tricep Extension with Right HG IR
Same bolster setup. Tubing knotted above the right hand, both hands in the loop. Blades down, palms toward the feet. Left hand stable. Straighten the right elbow. Then turn the right hand toward the mat. Back of the right shoulder blade and arm, then front of the right shoulder. Hold 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Supine: Supine Resisted Right HG IR with Left HG ER
Tubing above the right arm and below the left. Arms on bolsters, elbows 90. Blades down, elbows into the bolsters. Right hand down, left hand up. Front of right shoulder, back of left shoulder. Hold 4-5 breaths. Return. Repeat 4 more times.

### Sidelying: Left Sidelying Weighted Right HG IR with Left FA IR
Left side, knees bent, head on pillows. Bolster under the left side and the right elbow. Weight in the right hand, elbow 90. Push the left hip down so the left abs leave the towel. Right blade down and back, right arm to shoulder level. Right knee ahead and turned up, right outside hip on. Right hand turns up, back of the right shoulder blade on. Left knee turns up toward the right, left inner thigh on. Inhale, then exhale and turn the right hand down. Inhale up, exhale down, 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Sidelying: Left Sidelying Resisted Right HG IR
Left side, back on the wall, knees bent. Tubing above the head. Bolster under the left side, pillows under the head. Right arm at shoulder level in the tubing. Left hip down, left abs off the towel. Right knee ahead and up, right outside hip on. Left knee up toward the right, left inner thigh on. Right elbow into the wall, blade down and back. Turn the right hand toward the feet. Front of the shoulder on. Hold 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Sidelying: Sidelying Trunk Lift
Left side first: hips and knees 90, prop on the left forearm, elbow under the shoulder. Abs on, right knee down into the left. Left blade down and back, lift the hip. Left abs, back of the left shoulder, right inner thigh. Right hand overhead, 5 breaths. Lower. Repeat 4 more times. Then the right side, mirrored: prop on the right forearm, left knee into the right, right blade down, left hand overhead.

### Sidelying: Three Point Stance (side not named)
Two setups. Do not assign a left or right.
Top leg in front, bottom leg behind. Prop on the bottom hand, elbow straight, hand and trunk between the legs. Tighten lower abs and the upper inner thigh by arching up. Top hand behind the head, blades down and together. Lift the hip and waist. Back of the shoulder blades, lower abs, top inner thigh. 4-5 breaths. Lower. Repeat 4 more times.
Other setup: bottom leg in front, top leg behind. Same prop. Tighten lower abs and the lower outside hip. Same blade cue and lift. Feel lower outside hip instead of the top inner thigh. 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Sidelying: Modified Three Point Stance (side not named)
Top leg forward, bottom leg back. Prop on the bottom forearm. Arch up and press both ankles into the mat. Top hand at the side, blades down and together. Lift the hip and waist. Back of the shoulder blades and lower abs. 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Seated: Seated Resisted Pull Downs with Right HG IR and Left HG ER
Chair, feet flat, knees at hip level. Tubing from above, ends at shoulder level in front of the face. Palms toward each other. Round the back, find the sit bones. Straighten the arms toward the hips. Right elbow stays straight, right palm turns away, thumb in. Left elbow 90, left hand turns out. Front of right shoulder and back of left shoulder blade. Hold 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Seated: Seated Resisted Right HG IR
Tubing from above into the right hand, right elbow at shoulder level. Round the back, sit bones. Right blade down and back, elbow into the wall. Turn the right hand down and reach the left hand toward the floor, sidebend left. Front of the right shoulder blade and left outer abs. Hold 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Prone: Prone Weighted Right Abducted HG ER with HG IR
Stomach, right arm can hang. Pillows under the stomach, towel under the forehead. Right arm at shoulder level with a weight, bolster under the right shoulder, left hand on the mat. Blades down and together. Turn the right hand up toward the head (back of the right shoulder blade). Then lower and turn the hand down toward the feet (front of the right shoulder). Hold 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Prone: Prone Resisted Weighted Abducted Right HG IR
Same prone setup. Tubing from a door in front and to the right, plus a weight, in the right hand. Bolster under the upper arm. Blades together. Turn the right hand down toward the feet against the tubing. Front of the shoulder. Hold 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

### Standing: Standing Supported Left AF IR with Upper Extremity Dynamic Resistance
Four tubing directions, each with the right elbow at 90, right foot off the ground, 4-5 breaths.
1. Tubing behind: slight forward reach.
2. Tubing on the right: slight turn in.
3. Tubing in front: slight pull back.
4. Tubing on the left: slight turn out.
Reference: left abs, left heel. The sheet prints "right right arm" on the front-tubing line. Keep it as a right-arm drill.

### Standing: Standing Quadratus Lumborum and Intercostal Stretch
Face a doorway. Right hand inside the frame, palm out. Feet shoulder width, right foot behind. Round the back, turn the trunk right, left arm hangs. Shift toward the right hip and take up slack in the right arm. Stretch in the right chest. Rotate the hips right. Stretch just above the right hip bone. Hold 4-5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.
