# Mindbody staff book and cancel

Use this when staff needs to book or cancel a named client on the Mindbody **staff website** (not the consumer Book Now page, not the API). Also use it to pull a waitlisted client into a class, check whether requested spots are open, or block an hour on a coach’s appointment column for an evaluation.

## Client asks to book (default path)

When a client/parent asks to be booked into specific sessions (Numa or email):

1. Check those exact day/times on Classes (or Appointments). Note Sign In (n/capacity) and waitlist `(k)`. If both Semi-Private and Athletic Development run at that time, prefer Athletic Development for athletes unless they named Semi-Private.
2. If the spots are **open**, stop and ask Ryne to confirm the named client and the exact sessions (day, time, class or appointment) before booking. Do **not** ask the client whether they want those sessions. Before the click, the Mindbody profile must match the requester on phone or email, not just name; if two profiles match, stop and report.
2a. After booking, re-open the Sign In roster (or the client's Schedule tab) and confirm the client name, date and time are there. Report what you read back. If it does not match, stop and report; do not book again.
3. Draft a confirmation in chat: `Hi, FirstName.` then that the sessions are booked (times). Do not type into Numa/Gmail send boxes. Nothing **sends** until Ryne says send on that exact draft.
4. If a spot is **full**, do not book that slot (no overbook unless Ryne says to). Draft that it’s full and name the next open option if you checked one.
5. Chief of Staff saying book is not enough for a book that the client did not request. Cancels, waitlist pulls, and eval blocks still need Ryne to name the person and say to do it.

## Sign in

Staff login is `clients.mindbodyonline.com` → Staff sign-in, or the studio’s staff home. Session persists in the browser. If a staff login form appears, hand the desktop to the user. Never type their password.

## Search a client

Header **Find a client** is the fastest path to a profile. Sidebar **Clients** is the directory. Profiles have a **Schedule** tab of upcoming visits. There is usually **no Book button on the profile** - booking happens from Classes or Appointments.

## Book a class

1. Sidebar → **Classes**. Date picker + Day/Week/Month. Each row is one occurrence (time, Sign In count/capacity, name, teacher).
2. Click **Sign In (x/y)** for that occurrence.
3. On the roster, use **Sign in a client or add a new one → Search for client**.
4. Selecting the client from the dropdown creates the visit. Stop before that click until Ryne has confirmed the named client and slot, then complete it and read the roster back (step 2a above).
5. **Buy** on a row means they need a payment option first. **Adv. Register** is multi-occurrence. Do not Buy/charge unless Ryne says to.

## Pull someone from the waitlist into the class

1. On the class list, waitlist count is the small `(k)` after `Sign In (n/capacity)` - easy to miss.
2. Open **Sign In** for that occurrence and scroll to the **Waitlist** section under the roster.
3. Each waitlist row has **Add to class**, **Buy**, **Account**, and a red **X** (removes them from the waitlist only).
4. **Add to class** is the last click and may run immediately with no extra confirm. Stop before it until Ryne says yes.
5. Staff can add from the waitlist even when the class is full (overbook). Client-side promotion still needs an open spot. **Buy** first if they have no pass.

## Book an appointment

1. Sidebar → **Appointments**. Grid: columns = staff, rows = time slots. Use the right staff column and date.
2. Click an empty slot (or **+Add**). Left panel: **Book Appointments → Search for client**.
3. After the client: service type, staff, date/time, location, pricing option, then **Book/Save**.
4. Stop at client search (or before Book/Save) until Ryne says yes. After Book/Save, re-open the grid and confirm client, staff, date and time.
5. Some blocks are **unavailability** (not appointments). Do not treat those as client bookings.

## Block an hour for an evaluation

Evals are **unavailability** on the coach’s column, not a client appointment. Always make them **1 hour**.

1. Appointments → empty slot on the correct staff column.
2. Click **Mark as unavailable**.
3. Set **Reason** to `Eval – Name`, dates (usually Single day), and **Time** to a full hour. The end time defaults to only 15 minutes - change it.
4. **Hide from public** stays on. **Save** is the last confirm click. Cancel/close if Ryne has not said yes.
5. Existing eval blocks may be weekly recurring. Edit via **Edit this unavailability**. Delete via **Remove unavailability**. Watch for this-occurrence vs series.

### After every eval: sync Google Calendar

Mindbody does **not** always auto-sync to Google Calendar. After saving an eval, always:

**Staff → [that coach] → Share** under the Google Calendar integration box.

Do this every time an eval is added. Do not skip it.

## Cancel a class reservation (one client)

1. Classes → **Sign In** for that occurrence → find the client row.
2. Red **✕** on the left removes them (confirm dialog). That usually returns the session.
3. **Late Cancel** checkbox records a late cancel and typically deducts/charges. Use only when Ryne wants a late cancel.
4. Do **not** use the class row kebab **Cancel or delete class** - that kills the whole occurrence for everyone.
5. Alternate: client profile → **Schedule** → Actions on that visit.

## Cancel an appointment

Appointments → that date/staff → click the appointment block → Cancel (then confirm), or client profile → Schedule → Actions.

## Stop points

- Cancel / waitlist pull / eval: still need Ryne’s yes on the named person.
- Client-requested open-slot book: stop before the Sign In select until Ryne confirms the named client and slot; after booking, read the roster back; do not send the confirmation text until Ryne says send.
- Appointment book (non-client-request): client-search panel, or before Book/Save.
- Eval block: unavailability form open, do not Save until Ryne says yes.
- Class cancel: before the red ✕ confirm (and before Late Cancel).

## Gotchas

- Confirm location/studio and the correct staff column. An empty-slot click targets that column’s staff.
- Capacity and waitlist. Expired pricing options or waivers can block sign-in. Amy-style “app won’t book until payment” often still works from staff Sign In - if Buy blocks, stop and report.
- 24-hour cancel policy may charge if Late Cancel is used. When Ryne just says “cancel,” default to a regular remove unless they say late cancel or the visit is inside the policy window - then ask.
- An AI promo modal can block the Classes page; dismiss it with the X.
- After an eval, Google Calendar will be stale until you hit Staff → coach → Share.
- Never send Numa/email confirmations until Ryne says send on that exact draft. Booking the visit ≠ sending the text.
