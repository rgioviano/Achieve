# PRI fundamentals glossary

Short definitions for advisor use. Pattern treatment stays in the pattern skills. Cutoffs and how-to stay in `eval-cutoffs`. Course theory stays in the course skills. Citations are PRI manuals.

## Chains and positions

- **AIC (Anterior Interior Chain):** Two tracts (L and R) from costal cartilage/bone of ribs 7-12 to the lateral patella, fibular head, and lateral tibial condyle. Core members: diaphragm + psoas, with iliacus, TFL, biceps femoris, vastus lateralis. Influence respiration, trunk/ribcage/spine rotation, and LE position. (**PRI**, Postural Respiration, Aug 2019 rev., polyarticular chains.)
- **Left AIC:** The typical compensatory pattern: left hemi-pelvis anterior and forward, left AF ER / right AF IR. Open `left-aic`.
- **Right AIC:** Desired alternating opposite: left stance in Left AF IR (and right AF ER). Not a first gym program while Left AIC / Right BC / PEC tests are still positive. (**PRI**, Myokinematic Restoration, 2017, stance / pattern definitions.)
- **BC (Brachial Chain):** Two tracts over the anterior pleural/cervical area, ribs 4-7 and xiphoid to occiput, mandible, and coracoid. Members: triangularis sterni, SCM, scalenes, pec minor, intercostals, pharynx/anterior neck. Open `right-bc` for the typical right-sided pattern.
- **Right BC / Left BC:** Right BC is opposed by right PEC, lower trap, serratus, external rib rotators, plus left IO. Left BC is opposed by left PEC, lower trap, serratus, external rib rotators, plus right IO. (**PRI**, Postural Respiration, polyarticular chains.)
- **B BC (bilateral BC):** Bilateral shoulder horizontal abduction <45° can be a bilateral BC and can also be a bilateral PEC. If +B ADT and +B PADT, PEC or Patho PEC owns the first block. Do not add a mirrored left BC program. (**PRI**, Postural Respiration, p.38; already a gate in `right-bc` / `pec`.)
- **PEC (Posterior Exterior Chain):** Posterior extensors / QL system that closes the posterior mediastinum and drives bilateral extension when both sides are on. “Behind every PEC is a Left AIC.” Open `pec`.
- **Patho PEC:** Same bilateral extension bias as PEC, but the pelvic diaphragm is descended, bulged, and high resting tone. Can toe-touch or deep-squat while +B ADT and +B PADT remain. Open `patho-pec`.
- **R TMCC (Right Temporomandibular Cervical Chain):** Named on the Postural Respiration chain table (levator scapulae, cervical colli, etc.). Full Cervical Revolution / cranio-mandibular treatment is not in the Desktop course-manual set. For neck/FHP in the gym, use Anterior Neck Inhibition after ZOA (`pri-right-bc-treatment-guidelines`, PR p.76). TMJ-cervical manuals stay a clinician / future-manual gap.
- **ZOA (Zone of Apposition):** The cylindrical portion of the diaphragm apposed to the inner aspect of the lower rib cage, from the costal insertion up to the costophrenic angle. In standing quiet breathing about ¼-⅓ of total rib-cage surface; at rest overall about 30%. Controlled by the abdomen/obliques. Elevated, externally rotated anterior ribs decrease ZOA (left in Left AIC, both sides in PEC). Active established ZOA example: standing reach (fingers to toes) while inhaling with anterior mediastinal compression and posterior mediastinal expansion. (**PRI**, Postural Respiration, ZOA section.)
- **AF / FA:** Acetabulum-on-femur vs femur-on-acetabulum. Left AIC work is not “more clamshells.” Open `pri-myokinematic-restoration`.
- **Inlet / outlet (IP, IS, IsP, SI):** Pelvic inlet and outlet language from Pelvis Restoration. Left AIC and PEC maps live in `pri-pelvis-restoration`.
- **Posterior mediastinum:** Opens the transverse plane when flexed; limits thoracic rotation when closed/extended in PEC. In Right BC: more closed/anterior on the left, more opened/posterior on the right. DATES mnemonic in `pri-postural-respiration`.

## Treatment words

- **Reposition:** Restore a more neutral pelvis/thorax (best-set non-manuals) before strengthening into the pattern.
- **Retrain / inhibit vs facilitate:** After repositioning, use the printed inhibition or facilitation list that matches the limiter (Myo pp.56-58 programs; PR pp.68-69 and 75-77; Pelvis hierarchies).
- **Restore:** Upright, alternating, reciprocal activity only when the tests that gate that step are negative.
- **Non-manual vs manual:** Gym work is non-manual. Superior T4, subclavius, infraclavicular, Sibson, intercostal, pelvic-floor, and other hands-on techniques are clinician-only.
- **Refer:** If an honest printed ladder does not change the tests, stop adding volume or cuff work and refer to a PRI-trained clinician (PRC/PRT).
