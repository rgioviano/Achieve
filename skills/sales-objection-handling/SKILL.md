# Sales objection handling

## Method
1. **Clarify first, never rebut.** Repeat their word back in a confused tone and pause: "Think it over?" Let them explain the real concern.
2. **Probe.** "How do you mean exactly?" / "What part feels like you'd want to think through?"
3. **3A.** Acknowledge ("That makes sense"), Associate (others felt the same and decided on the goal, not the number; real only), Ask (a question that returns to their stated goal or consequence).
4. **Diagnose the earlier step.** Price = consequence didn't land, go back to their cost of waiting. "Think about it" = commitment skipped, ask the feel question. "Send me info" = no real connection, go back to connecting.
5. **Belief-breaking** for DIY objections: see [Mozi sales frameworks](sand-workflow:mozi-sales-frameworks) section 6.

## Scripts
- **Think it over:** "Think about it?" (pause) "What part feels like you'd want to think through?"
- **Too expensive / it's a lot:** "A lot compared to what you were expecting, or a lot for what [athlete] would get?" Then: "That makes sense, most parents feel that way at first. If cost weren't a factor, is this what you'd want for [athlete]?" Then back to their own consequence. Last resort: shorter-term downsell from the pricing sheet.
- **Need to talk to spouse / other parent:** "Of course. What do you think they'll want to know, so I can answer it now?" Offer a short follow-up that same week with both.
- **Season's not over:** In-season once-a-week option: keep spot, keep strength, bill drops ($100 Build / $200 Performance, same term).
- **Timing / start later:** "Later?" (pause) Then: "How important is it to get this started this off-season, as opposed to later?" Use their date (tryouts, season). Accept an honest no.
- **Send me info:** "Happy to. What would you be looking for in it?" Then reconnect to why they came in.

## Guardrails
No fear tactics about injuries. No invented stats or outcomes. Accept a real "not now" and move to [Sales follow-up after eval](sand-workflow:sales-follow-up-after-eval).
