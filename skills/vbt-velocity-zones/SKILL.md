# VBT velocity zones
Source: Output Sports guide (Bryan Mann zones, adapted from Weakley et al.): https://www.outputsports.com/blog/what-are-vbt-zones-a-beginners-guide-to-velocity-based-training

## Zones
- Light-loaded power (speed-strength): lower 1.1-1.5 m/s at 40-60% 1RM; upper 0.8-1.1 m/s at 30-50%. Fast force, SSC.
- Heavy-loaded power (strength-speed): lower 0.75-1.0 m/s at 60-80%; upper 0.5-0.75 m/s at 50-70%. Move heavy load fast.
- General strength: lower 0.6-0.8 m/s at 75-85%; upper 0.41-0.55 m/s at 75-85%. Muscle, tendon stiffness, tissue tolerance.
- Maximal strength: lower 0.3-0.6 m/s at 85%+; upper 0.17-0.4 m/s at 85%+.

## How to use
1. Pick lower vs upper zones (upper is slower; shorter bar path).
2. Match the target velocity to a zone; if it straddles two, interpolate (faster end = lower %).
3. Multiply by the 1RM or working max for a starting load; round to plates.
4. Adjust from measured speed: too slow, drop 5-10%; too fast, add 5-10%. Every rep at max intent.

## Caveats
- Built mostly on back squat and deadlift; other lifts and athletes vary, and each lift has its own minimal velocity threshold. Starting points, not rules.
- A training max below true 1RM means faster speeds at a given %.
