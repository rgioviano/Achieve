# PEC pattern (restoration)

Bilateral PEC pelvis: both anterior inlets and both posterior outlets biased into extension. Behind every PEC is a Left AIC. You still get to Left AIC facilitation later. You do not start there. Bilateral inhibition comes before unilateral work.

If they can touch their toes or full-squat and both ADT and PADT are positive, open Patho PEC. The first three repositioning titles are shared. The path is not.

## Best set, in this order

Bilateral inhibition only. One or two, earliest position first.

1. Standing wall supported reach. Both arms forward and down. This is the opening reach.
2. Supine 90-90 hip lift in passive FA IR with balloon. Feet wider than the hips. No march.
3. Modified all-four belly lift, after the belly lift is already on.

Houglum's pick when PEC and bilateral BC are both present: standing integration #23, wall reach with balloon. That is still bilateral inhibition, not a Right BC tricep drill.

If they cannot do the 90-90, Pelvis Restoration sends you to the Patho step-2 sheets (wall passive FA IR balloon, wall-and-chair IO/TA, squatting bar reverse reach, non-modified all-four belly lift, seated bar reach). Those stay inhibition. They are not a loaded squat.

Left AIC facilitation waits until inlet extension is maximized and the Hruska abduction lift is at least 1/5.

## Who this pattern is

- ADT positive both sides. Left-only is Left AIC.
- Standing reach greater than 0 inches (cannot touch toes). 0 inches is a Patho test.
- PADT positive both sides.
- PART negative both sides. Positive on one or both sides is a Patho test.
- Functional squat under 3/5. Greater than 3/5 is a Patho test.
- Hruska Abduction Lift under 3/5. This does not separate PEC from Left AIC or Patho PEC.

Level 3 squat is bottom below the knees, heels down, trunk flexed. Bottom-to-heels or a maximal heel-centered squat with the drop tests still positive is Patho PEC.

A bilateral shoulder horizontal-abduction limit under 45 degrees both sides can be this bilateral PEC. Own the pelvis first. Opposition muscle named in Postural Respiration: abdominal obliques.

## Position order

1. Standing Wall Supported Reach. Bilateral arms, rounded back, low back on the wall.
2. Supine 90-90 Hip Lift in Passive FA IR with Balloon. Feet wider than the hips. Both sides. No single-leg march.
3. Modified All Four Belly Lift. Hands alternate only after the belly lift is on.
4. If they cannot do 90-90, use the Patho step-2 list in that order. Do not invent a separate level-2 and level-3 reverse reach.
5. Left AIC alternating facilitation only after inlet extension is maximized, and only after the Hruska Abduction Lift is at least 1/5.
6. Upright left single-leg frontal plane after a 3/5 Hruska Abduction Lift, then right, then integration.

### Standing Wall Supported Reach

Heels 7 to 10 inches from the wall. Ball between the knees. Round the back, low back flat on the wall. Slight squat, squeeze the ball. Inhale. Exhale and reach both arms forward and down so the upper back comes off the wall. Low back stays flat. Inhale into the upper back. 4 to 5 breaths. Stand through the heels. Repeat 4 more times.

This is bilateral on purpose. Do not swap in the left-hip-back wall squat on day one.

### 90-90 Hip Lift in Passive FA IR with Balloon

Feet wider than the hips. Ball between the knees. Exhale into the pelvic tilt. Blow the balloon, pause 3 seconds, tongue up. Inhale and relax the tilt. Blow and redo the tilt. 4 to 5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times. Do not march one leg. Do not reach one arm yet. Passive FA IR is the wide-foot position, not an active IR cue with the lat.

### Modified All Four Belly Lift

Hands and knees, back rounded, weight forward until the nose is over the fingertips. Lift one hand without twisting the trunk. 4 to 5 breaths each side. The hand lift is an abdominal test, not a bird dog.

### After repositioning

Negative bilateral ADT, PADT, and PART: Myokinematic Restoration and Postural Respiration.

Still positive, or negative ADT with positive PADT: stay in Pelvis Restoration. Maximize inlet extension. Then Left AIC facilitation, gated at Hruska Abduction Lift 1/5. Do not run that on a Patho screen.

Squat breathing, if a squat is already allowed, follows Thomsen, not the Fitness and Movement page. Ascend on an exhale. Inhale-down is the earned third variant. A PEC who cannot toe-touch is not in variant 3.

## PEC positional guidelines (gym cues)

From Fitness & Movement 2018 pp.290-292 (also 2020 appendix ~pp.105-107). Floor-usable position cues for PEC athletes; not manual techniques.

- **Supine:** fully exhale to flex the T8 midzone via rib IR; close the infrasternal angle; reach both arms to the ceiling (left inhale, right exhale); inhibit pecs and anterior neck. Ball between the knees for adductors - watch do not overuse the right adductor.
- **Sitting:** keep the sternum facing forward (not upward); feel the back of the ribs rest against the chair.
- **Standing:** may place the left foot behind the right; feel left heel and right arch; reach both arms away to promote thoracic flexion; encourage arm swing.

## Hard stops

- Do not start with hemibridge marches, left glute med, and retro stairs.
- Do not call a toe-touch or a deep squat a PEC success.
- Do not left-reach first. Early reaches are both arms forward and down.
- Do not add right tricep, cuff, or true overhead because the shoulders look rounded.
- Do not progress squat depth to fix a reach that is still over 0 inches.

## Handoff

Toe-touch or deep squat with bilateral ADT and PADT: Patho PEC. Stop facilitation. Left PADT remains at a 1/5 abduction lift: Left AIC skill, hemi 90-90 if the frontal plane is the limiter. Thorax still limited: Right BC, and left serratus before right tricep if apical expansion is still asymmetric. Lift order is Fitness and Movement, after this gate.
