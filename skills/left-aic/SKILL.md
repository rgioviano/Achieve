# Left AIC pattern (restoration floor)

This is the Postural Restoration Institute restoration floor for a classic Left AIC pelvis and left Zone of Apposition. It is not Fitness and Movement programming.

Run the Patho PEC screen before any technique here. If they can toe-touch or deep-squat and both ADT and PADT are positive bilaterally, stop. Open Patho PEC.

## Best set, in this order

Use one or two from the earliest position they have not earned. Do not start at standing.

1. Supine 90-90 Supported Hip Lift with Hemibridge. The non-manual CD puts this before the balloon.
2. Supine 90-90 Hip Lift with Right Arm Reach and Balloon. Right arm on the exhale. Not a left reach.
3. Right sidelying respiratory left adductor pull-back.
4. Left sidelying right glute max, or left sidelying knee-toward-knee, only when the Hruska abduction lift is at least 2/5 (Houglum consult, 2024-06-02).
5. Right sidelying hemi 90-90 only if the frontal-plane pelvis is the limiter.
6. If honest 90-90 does not change them, stop repeating it and use the PRIVY-281 ladder below. Do not invent a new first drill.
7. Standing supported passive left AF IR with right trunk rotation only after left AF IR and left IO/TA hold. Right reach before left reach.

When Left AIC and Right BC are both on the table, Houglum's one drill that hits both is right sidelying apical expansion, and it still comes after the left zone of apposition, not instead of steps 1 to 3.

Pain: traditional floor work before and after training, and between heavy extension sets. No pain: do not put this in the warm-up. The preferred pattern is what they get stronger in. Save upright alternating work for the last 10 to 15 minutes (Ryne's integration note, 2024-06-05, and the Houglum consult).

## Who this pattern is

Pelvis Restoration when the Adduction Drop Test is positive on the left, not as a bilateral pair:

- Adduction Drop Test positive left.
- Standing reach greater than 0 inches (cannot touch toes).
- Pelvic Ascension Drop Test positive left.
- Passive Abduction Raise Test positive right.
- Functional squat under 3/5.
- Hruska Abduction Lift under 3/5.

PEC is bilateral ADT and PADT, reach still over 0 inches, PART negative, squat under 3/5. Patho PEC can toe-touch or squat over 3/5 with those drop tests still positive. A pretty toe-touch is not a Left AIC win.

## Position order

1. Supine 90-90. Hemibridge first, then balloon with a right-arm reach.
2. Sidelying. Right sidelying left adductor pull-back, then left sidelying right glute max.
3. Right sidelying hemi 90-90 only when the frontal-plane pelvis is the limiter.
4. PRIVY-281 ladder only if supine 90-90 does not change them.
5. Standing only with a right reach or right trunk rotation, after left AF IR and left IO/TA hold.
6. Upright left single-leg, then right, only after PADT is negative and the Hruska abduction lift is 3/5.

## Supine scripts

90-90 Supported Hip Lift with Hemibridge: feet on the wall, hips and knees 90. Inhale. Exhale and pelvic-tilt, tailbone slightly off, low back flat, heels digging. Keep the tilt with the left leg and straighten the right leg. Take the right leg on and off the wall. Feel the back of the left thigh. 3 sets of 10.

90-90 Hip Lift with Right Arm Reach and Balloon: same setup, 4 to 6 inch ball between the knees, balloon in the left hand, right arm reaches toward the ceiling on the exhale. Pause 3 seconds, tongue up. Four breaths, then let the balloon air out. Repeat 4 more times.

## Sidelying scripts

Right sidelying respiratory left adductor pull-back: right side lying, feet on the wall, hips and knees 90, back rounded. Bolster between the feet, towel between the knees, left knee lower than the left hip. Push the right foot into the wall. Inhale and pull the left leg back. Exhale and squeeze the left knee into the towel for 3 seconds. 4 to 5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

Left sidelying right glute max: left side lying, feet on the wall, towel under the ankles and under the left abs. Press the left hip down. Shift the right hip forward. Turn the right knee out, left knee stays down. Hold 4 to 5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

## 90-90 non-responder ladder (PRIVY-281)

Only after the supine work above fails. Order:

1. Right sidelying left adductor pull-back with balloon.
2. All four left posterior mediastinum expansion in left AF IR.
3. Wall supported squat with balloon, then wall supported squat with right apical expansion.
4. Stair short seated balloon, wall short seated left arm reach with balloon, kneeling bar reach. The left-arm reach sits here, not in front of the right-arm balloon.
5. Standing resisted alternating respiratory reach, then forward alternating reciprocal resisted reach.
6. Late left AIC stance with right arm reach.

If that ladder does not change anything, stop and refer. Do not add cuff work.

## Hard stops

- Do not open this skill on a Patho PEC screen.
- Do not lead with left glute med or retro stairs on that screen.
- Do not reach with the left arm on the exhale until left AF IR and left IO/TA are on.
- Do not stretch the left hamstring as the fix for a short-looking straight-leg raise.
- Do not train humeral internal rotation with the lat.

## Handoff

Negative left ADT and negative left PADT: Myokinematic Restoration if the remaining problem is femoral rotation. Left PADT stays positive: stay here, hemi 90-90. Pelvis holds and the Right BC cluster is still positive: Right BC. Bilateral drop tests: PEC if they cannot toe-touch, Patho PEC if they can.
