## 1. Test cluster (track + / −)

Before and after every stage, recheck this Right BC cluster:

| Test | Typical patterned finding |
|---|---|
| Left horizontal abduction | + (limited) |
| Left cervical axial rotation | + (limited) |
| Left humeral glenoid flexion | + (limited) |
| Right apical expansion | + (limited) *or* − R / + L in Superior T4 syndrome branch |
| Right humeral glenoid internal rotation | + (limited) *or* − (≤80° or better) when routing to R subscap |

**Coach translation:** these are screening language for warm-up / tertiary choices — not a clinical diagnosis. Pair with ADT / trunk rotation / standing reach (ZOA) from course skills.

## 2. Always start: ZOA Abdominal Non-Manual Techniques

Handout entry box — restore Zone of Apposition / abdominal opposition **before** scapular muscle programs:

1. **90-90 Hip Lift with Right Arm Reach and Balloon**
2. **PRI Wall Supported Squat with Balloon**
3. **Left Sidelying Knee Toward Knee with Balloon**
4. **Right Sidelying Right Apical Expansion with Left Arm Reach and Left Adductor**
5. **Standing Supported Passive Left AF IR with Right Trunk Rotation**

Achieve equivalents often used in warm-up: 90-90 balloon family, wall-supported squat/reach with balloon or long exhale, left sidelying knee-to-knee / adductor patterns, right apical expansion sidelying with left reach + left adductor sense.

**Gate:** do not progress R low trap / tricep isolation while the athlete is still flared, extended, and failing apical / horizontal abduction screens without first earning ZOA.

## 3. Decision tree (handout + appendix overview)

### Branch A — All tests − after ZOA NM
→ **Right Low Trap & Right Tricep Program** (primary neuromotor next step when thorax/BC tests clear).

### Branch B — All or some tests remain + after ZOA NM
→ **Clinician path:** Left AIC and Sternal – With Active Pelvic Tilt **manual** techniques.  
→ If still +: **Right Superior T4 Manual Technique**.  
→ Then recheck:

#### B1 — All tests − after Superior T4 path
→ Stabilize spine with **Left Low Trap & Left Serratus Anterior Program** *(prior to initiating Right Low Trap & Right Tricep Program per handout)*.

#### B2 — All or some tests remain + **and** − Right Apical Expansion / + Left Apical Expansion
→ **Right Subclavius Manual Technique** (Superior T4 syndrome path — clinician).  
→ When all tests − → **Left Low Trap & Left Serratus** (stabilize), then later R low trap/tricep as indicated.

### Branch C — Impingement & Instability consideration (appendix overview)
**All tests −** but **insufficient right scapular-thoracic function** (reach forward drops R shoulder or IR's R UE; or prone R serratus weaker):
→ Clinician: Left AIC manuals → Right BC manuals emphasizing **Infraclavicular Pump, Right Sibson, Right Intercostal**  
→ Then **Right Serratus Anterior** program.

### Branch D — Cluster still + but **− Right HG IR** (≤80° or better available)
→ Clinician: PRI Left AIC + Right BC manuals to thorax neutrality  
→ Then **Right Subscapularis** program (true HG IR independent of lat).

### Branch E — All tests − **and** sufficient R ST function
Patient can reach forward with R UE in supine, prone, and left sidelying with tests remaining −  
→ **Standing Right Low Trap and Right Tricep** (late positional demand).

## 4. Muscle program menus (from handout)

### Right Low Trap & Right Tricep Program (examples listed)
- Supine Weighted Tricep Curls  
- Supine Resisted Right Tricep Extension with Right HG IR  
- Seated Resisted Alternating Tricep Pull Downs  
- Seated Right Scapula Pull Backs with Right HG Horizontal Abduction  
- Long Seated Supported Press Down with Abdominals  
- Paraspinal Release with Left Hamstring  
- Standing Resisted Alternating Tricep Pull Downs  
- Left Stance in Left AF IR Position from the Left AIC Pattern with Right Upper Extremity Resisted Reach  
- Left Sidelying Resisted Right Serratus Punch with Right Trunk Rotation  
- Sidelying Trunk Lift (Right)

**Acquired action (Postural / NM workshop):** R low trap + tricep retract and posterior-tilt scapula on a stabilized spine with left abs; promote thoracic flexion; deactivate lat dominance.

**Position progression (neuro-motor demand ↑):** Supine → All Four → Seated → Long Seated → Standing → Sidelying → Prone.

### Left Low Trap & Left Serratus Anterior Program (stabilize spine — handout says prior to R LT/Tricep when coming off manuals)
- 90-90 Supported Wall Leg Lift with Alternating Resisted Arm Reach  
- Seated Resisted Serratus Punch with Left Hamstrings  
- Standing Serratus Squat  
- All Four Belly Lift Reach  
- Three Point Stance

**Acquired action:** L low trap = mid-spinal rotator to the right; L serratus stabilizes thorax for right thoracic rotation / left rib IR and desired kyphosis (reach arms forward **and** ribs back).

### Right Serratus Anterior (I&I / ST insufficiency branch)
Use after neutrality + clinician infraclavicular-family work when R ST strength/position still fails. Goal: stabilize R scapula for R arm reach **without** left trunk rotation / right rib retraction compensation.

### Right Subscapularis (− R HG IR branch)
Only after thorax neutrality. Train true HG IR **independent of lat** — critical for swimmers and overhead athletes (link I&I).

## 5. Clinician-only pathways (know, don't coach as gym drills)

| Manual | Role in tree |
|---|---|
| L AIC + Sternal with active pelvic tilt | Persistently + tests after ZOA NM |
| Right Superior T4 | Persistently + after L AIC manuals; also assessment for Superior T4 syndrome |
| Right Subclavius | − R apical / + L apical after Superior T4 (Sup T4 syndrome) |
| Infraclavicular Pump (+ Opposition), Right Sibson, Right Intercostal | Insufficient R ST despite − tests (I&I handoff) |

S&C response when athlete is stuck on these branches: keep ZOA / inhibition / earlier NM programs, refer to PRI-trained clinician, do **not** load cuff isolation on a patterned thorax.

## 6. Inhibition menus (when BC stays sticky)

Use when: limited R apical expansion; limited L horizontal abduction; deep inspiration drives L rib flare / L back extension / R SCM / right lateralize; chronic R upper trap / SCM / QL / lat / paraspinal / hip-flexor tension.

Examples from Postural Respiration inhibition logic (coach-usable):
- Sidelying Swiss Ball Inhibition  
- R Sidelying R Apical Expansion with Balloon (± L FA IR)  
- Standing QL and Intercostal Stretch  
- Late Left AIC Stance with Right Arm Reach  
- All Four Intercostal and Latissimus Inhibition  
- L Sidelying L Flexed FA Adduction with R Glute Max and R Trunk Rotation  

---

## 7. What NOT to do

- Skip ZOA abdominal NM and jump to R low trap curls / band pull-aparts.  
- Train "weak serratus" or "tight shoulder" before ribcage under the scapula is addressed.  
- Use lat-dominated IR / pull patterns when the goal is R subscap.  
- Start standing / sidelying / prone R LT-tricep variants before supine/all-four versions succeed.  
- Improvise Superior T4 / Subclavius / Infraclavicular techniques in S&C.  
- Treat I&I ST failure the same as "all tests +" — different branch (R serratus after clinician ST manuals).

---

## 8. Achieve programming translation

1. Screen: L horiz abd, L cervical rot, L HG flex, R apical, R HG IR + ADT / reach.  
2. Warm-up: ZOA list (balloon 90-90, wall squat balloon, sidelying apical + adductor).  
3. If screens clear → R low trap / tricep family (start supine/seated).  
4. If screens sticky → more inhibition + L serratus / L low trap stabilize; escalate clinician if manuals indicated.  
5. Swimmers / OH: bias lat inhibition, wall reach / all-four belly lift reach, then R serratus / R subscap only after neutrality language.  
6. Pair extension-heavy lifts with flexion/reach or exhalation-biased breathing check.

Open [PRI Postural Respiration](sand-workflow:pri-postural-respiration) for ZOA theory; [PRI Impingement and Instability](sand-workflow:pri-impingement-instability) when ST function fails despite − BC tests.
