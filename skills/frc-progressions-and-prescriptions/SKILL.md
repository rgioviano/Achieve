# FRC progressions and prescriptions

Source: Achieve Google Doc *Functional Range Conditioning (FRC) Progressions* (Drive). Full cleaned text: `/home/box/agent-data/shared/sc-reference/frc-progressions.txt`. Do not store or surface FAS member logins from the doc.

Ryne overlay: methods generally go **in order**. **CARs** maintain mobility / maximize available space. **PAILs/RAILs** increase mobility. **PRH → PRLO → Hover → ERR** build strength and control at end range.

Swimmer dryland context: [Swim dryland programming](sand-workflow:swim-dryland-programming) (swimmer hovers / HA for fly and catch ownership). PRI pathomechanics: [PRI Swimming An In-Depth Look](sand-workflow:pri-swimming-in-depth).

## Session placement
- CARs: after PRI techniques, before dynamic warm-up.
- PAILs/RAILs and end-range work: mobility / prep or dedicated end-range block - not competing with high-CNS primary lifts in the same slot.

## Ladder (order)
1. **CARs** - maintain / express available ROM
2. **PAILs/RAILs** - expand usable ROM + tissue adaptation
3. **PRH** → **PRLO** → **Hover** → **ERR** - teach the nervous system to own the new range

PAILs/RAILs gain the range; end-range loading solidifies control. CARs help keep what you own. Cramping at end range usually means the tissue is unused to the request - regress volume/angle, don't force.

---

## 1. Controlled Articular Rotations (CARs)

**Why:** Joint-only rotation on a stable body. Stress the capsule through its available envelope. Not a ROM-gaining tool ("CARs are not going to fix CARs").

**Cue:** Inhale, trap air low abs, shallow breath. Stabilize everything else. Outer limit of the circle; expand each rep. Dense air (honey/molasses).

**Rx:** 1 set × 2-3 reps per direction.

**Movement pool:**
- Spine: All Fours segmented cat/camel; tall kneeling T/L; standing T/L
- GH: tall kneeling; standing
- Scapula: tall kneeling or standing, arm @ scapular plane & 90° abduction
- Hip: all fours (knee on pad); half kneeling supported; standing supported

---

## 2. PAILs & RAILs (Progressive / Regressive Angular Isometric Loading)

**Why:** Stretch + isometric at progressive (PAILs) and regressive (RAILs) angles to expand ROM and strengthen newly acquired and shorter angles.

**Procedure (condensed):** Stretch position → active into stretch → passive hold → build irradiated isometric (PAILs) with low-ab brace → on relax, active RAILs deeper → hold new range → re-establish passive.

**Achieve Rx (overrides course 2-min passive):**
- Passive stretch **45-60s** (not 2 min)
- **1 set of 3** progressive holds, **5-7s** each, for both progressive and regressive angles

**Movement pool:**
- Spine: All Fours flexion/extension PAILs/RAILs
- Shoulder: All Fours Swiss ball flexion + dowel; ER + dowel; horizontal abduction; Bar hang scapular elevation
- Hip capsule: Seated 90/90 ER; Seated 90/90 IR; Seated sleeper (IR)

---

## 3. End-range strength / control (PALs/RALs ladder)

### Passive Range Holds (PRH)
- Find end range passively → remove assist → hold.
- Rep fails when joint drifts ~**15°** past the hold.
- Progress: short holds / more reps → longer holds / fewer reps  
  `5s×6 → 10s×3 → 15s×2 → 30s×1`

**Pool:** Seated 90/90 hip IR/ER; All Fours Swiss ball shoulder flex/habd; Swiss ball shoulder ER; Kneeling wrist extension (hang-clean catch); Prone shoulder IR (small prop OK).

### Passive Range Lift-Offs (PRLO)
- Leave room near PROM - don't start at absolute end.
- Progress closer to PROM while dropping reps; controlled eccentric failure, no cheat.  
  `20°×12-15 → 10°×8-10 → 5°×6-8 → at PROM (sticky)×3-5`

**Pool:** Seated 90/90 hip IR/ER; Sidelying hip ER; All Fours Swiss ball shoulder flex/habd; Swiss ball shoulder ER; Prone GH IR.

### Hovers
- Dynamic PRLO over barely-achievable targets.
- Progress zone divisions + reps: `6 → 8 → 10 → 12`. Controlled ecc failure.

**Pool:** Seated 90/90 hip IR or ER hover; Prone / tall kneeling / ½ kneeling swimmer hover (swimmers ~135° before elbow flex); Prone infinity swimmer hover; Prone shoulder flex or habd hover; Prone shoulder IR hover.

### End-Range Rotational Training (ERR)
- Small circles (~tennis ball) at end range (hips / GH / scap / spine as listed).
- Progress TUT and zone division:  
  `10s ea (20s) → 15s (30s) → 20s (40s) → 25-30s ea (50-60s)`

**Pool:** Prone shoulder flex/habd ERR; Kneeling or full straight-arm plank scapular rotation ERR; Pike scapular rotation ERR; Tall kneeling spinal ERR w/ med ball.

## Programming rules of thumb
- Pick **one joint / constraint** per session block unless prep volume is low.
- Don't stack PAILs/RAILs + heavy end-range of the same tissue on high-CNS days without a reason.
- Overhead athletes: prefer scapula CARs / hang elevation / Swiss-ball flex/ER patterns over aggressive vertical press end-range unless cleared.
- Swimmers: swimmer hovers and infinity hover are high-transfer for streamline / catch ownership (see swim-dryland-programming for when to prioritize them by stroke).
- If ROM is the limiter → PAILs/RAILs first. If they already have passive range but can't own it → skip to PRH/PRLO. If they own holds → Hovers/ERR.
