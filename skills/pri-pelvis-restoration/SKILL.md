## 1. Core theory / asymmetry model (this course)

Pelvis Restoration reframes the Left AIC / PEC problem in **pelvic inlet and outlet** language and ties the **pelvic diaphragm** to the respiratory diaphragm (reciprocal and alternating goals).

### Left AIC pelvic position summary (manual)
- **LEFT:** Inlet IP ER = IS IR = Outlet IsP IR = AF ER  
- **RIGHT:** Inlet IP IR = IS ER = Outlet IsP ER = AF IR  

(Translation for coaches: left hemi-pelvis is stuck in the pattern that blocks left AF IR / left frontal-plane shift; right side is the stance-biased opposite.)

### Neuro-muscular facilitation goals — to inhibit a Left AIC (inlet/outlet map)

**Pelvic Inlet**
| Side | Action | Key muscles (manual colors/labels) |
|---|---|---|
| Left | IP IR anterior inlet (Ext/Add/IR) | IO/TA |
| Right | IP ER anterior inlet (Flex/Abd/ER) | Rectus femoris, sartorius |
| Left | IS ER posterior inlet | Iliacus, gluteus medius |
| Right | IS IR posterior inlet | Gluteus maximus (superior fibers) |

**Pelvic Outlet**
| Side | Action | Key muscles |
|---|---|---|
| Left | IsP ER anterior outlet (Flex/Abd/ER) | Hamstring, IC adductor, obturator, iliococcygeus |
| Right | SI IR posterior outlet | Gluteus maximus (inferior fibers), piriformis, coccygeus |

### PEC pattern summary
- **Pelvic inlet:** Bilateral IP ER / IS IR — anterior sacrum flexed  
- **Pelvic outlet:** Bilateral IsP IR — posterior sacrum extended; coccyx flexed  
- Pelvic diaphragm **positionally descended, hypertonic, inflexible**  
Manual axiom: **"Behind every PEC is a Left AIC pattern."**

### Patho PEC position summary
Same bilateral inlet/outlet directional description as PEC, but pelvic diaphragm is **descended, bulged, and high resting tone** — a more pathologic pelvic-floor / outlet presentation. Patho-specific tests are bolded in the test matrix (below).

### Respiratory influence themes (course sections)
- Left AIC: hemi-diaphragm / hemi-pelvis coupling; left posterior outlet mediastinum expansion matters  
- PEC: bilateral anterior rib flare/ER; bilateral posterior mediastinum restriction  
- Patho PEC: same extension bias with greater pelvic diaphragm dysfunction  

Apical Expansion Test and Posterior Outlet Mediastinum Expansion Test appear in algorithms as respiratory companions to pelvis tests.

## 2. Patterns to recognize

### Left AIC (pelvis course)
+ Left ADT; often + Left PADT; + Right PART; Standing Reach > 0"; Functional Squat < 3/5; Hruska Abduction Lift < 3/5. Person cannot shift into left AF IR / control left outlet abduction and right outlet adduction in gait terms.

### PEC
+ Bilateral ADT; + Bilateral PADT; Standing Reach > 0"; PART − bilateral; Functional Squat < 3/5; Hruska Abd Lift < 3/5. Bilateral anterior inlet / posterior outlet bias; pelvic diaphragm descended/hypertonic.

### Patho PEC (* patho tests bolded in manual)
+ Bilateral ADT; **Standing Reach = 0"**; + Bilateral PADT; **PART + bilateral or unilateral**; **Functional Squat > 3/5**; Hruska Abd Lift < 3/5. Reach may look "better" while PART/squat pathology flags pelvic diaphragm / pathologic PEC.

### Ilio-sacral (IS) instability (Left AIC patients)
Separate hierarchy in the manual when IS instability overlays Left AIC — do not skip to aggressive alternating activity before inlet/outlet inhibition sequencing.

## 3. Assessment / special tests

### Battery (Pelvis Restoration Tests)
1. **Adduction Drop Test (ADT)** — Position  
2. **Standing Reach Test (SRT)** — Sagittal plane  
3. **Pelvic Ascension Drop Test (PADT)** — Frontal plane stance phase  
4. **Passive Abduction Raise Test (PART)** — Frontal plane swing phase  
5. **Functional Squat Test**  
6. **Hruska Abduction Lift Test** — also a Myokinematic measurement with pelvic interpretation  

Plus respiratory companions in algorithms: Left Posterior Outlet Mediastinum Expansion; Right Apical Expansion.

### ADT (detail)
Same sidelying procedure as other courses. Positive = femur will not adduct; reflective of osseous restriction from acetabular labral rim / related structures; usually left in Left AIC; bilateral in PEC/Patho PEC.

### Test matrices (when ADT is + left or bilateral)

**Left AIC**
| Test | Finding |
|---|---|
| ADT | + left |
| Standing Reach | > 0" |
| PADT | + left |
| PART | + right |
| Functional Squat | < 3/5 |
| Hruska Abd Lift | < 3/5 |

**PEC**
| Test | Finding |
|---|---|
| ADT | + bilateral |
| Standing Reach | > 0" |
| PADT | + bilateral |
| PART | − bilateral |
| Functional Squat | < 3/5 |
| Hruska Abd Lift | < 3/5 |

**Patho PEC**
| Test | Finding |
|---|---|
| ADT | + bilateral |
| *Standing Reach | 0" |
| PADT | + bilateral |
| *PART | + bilateral or unilateral |
| *Functional Squat | > 3/5 |
| Hruska Abd Lift | < 3/5 |

### Critical course-selection gates (PRI Identification page)
**Left AIC repositioning techniques** (candidates: 90-90 Supported Hip Lift with Hemibridge; Right Sidelying Respiratory Left Adductor Pull Back; Left Sidelying Right Glute Max) when + L ADT and + L PADT.

Decision:
- **− L ADT / + L PADT → Pelvis Restoration Program**  
- **− L ADT / − L PADT → Myokinematic Restoration Program**

**PEC / Patho PEC repositioning** (Standing Wall Supported Reach; 90-90 Hip Lift in Passive FA IR with Balloon; Modified All Four Belly Lift) when + B ADT and + B PADT.

Decision:
- **− B ADT / + B PADT → Pelvis Restoration Program**  
- **− B ADT / − B PADT → Myokinematic Restoration and Postural Respiration Program**

This is the clearest "which course owns this athlete today" rule in the three intros.

## 4. Inhibit vs facilitate (Pelvis composite)

From composite-of-courses tables (also printed in Postural manual):

**Pelvis Restoration — AIC chain (general)**  
- Facilitate **Pelvic Inlet:** proximal iliacus, sartorius, rectus femoris, IO/TA  
- Inhibit **Pelvic Inlet:** thoracic diaphragm, IO/EO, paravertebrals  
- Facilitate **Pelvic Outlet:** proximal obturator, iliococcygeus, puborectalis, pubococcygeus, glute max, piriformis, coccygeus  
- Inhibit **Pelvic Outlet:** adductor magnus, hamstrings  

**As related to Left AIC** (sided):  
Facilitate inlet L proximal iliacus, R sartorius, R rectus femoris, L IO/TA; outlet L proximal obturator, L iliococcygeus, L puborectalis/pubococcygeus, R glute max, R piriformis, R coccygeus.  
Inhibit inlet R thoracic hemi-diaphragm, R IO/EO, L paravertebrals; outlet R adductor magnus, R hamstrings, L piriformis, L coccygeus, L glute max.

**Goals language:** turn patterns from Left AIC "ON/OFF" maps toward **Right AIC alternating** (reciprocal and alternating pelvic and respiratory diaphragms). For PEC/Patho PEC, goals emphasize facilitation of abs with posterior pelvic tilt and proximal adductors into reciprocal inlet IP IR / outlet IsP ER bilaterally.

## 5. Repositioning logic, algorithms, hierarchies

### Left AIC Pattern — Pelvis Restoration Algorithm (test results cluster)
Typical positives: + L ADT, + SRT (can't touch toes), + L PADT, + R PART, <3/5 Functional Squat, <3/5 Hruska Abd Lift, + L Posterior Outlet Mediastinum Expansion, + R Apical Expansion.

**Repositioning menu**
1. 90-90 Supported Hip Lift with Hemibridge  
2. Right Sidelying Respiratory Left Adductor Pull Back  
3. Left Sidelying Right Glute Max  

**Branches**
- Repositions to **− L ADT, − L PADT, − R PART** → further assessment if still can't hold; may need other PRI courses  
- Remains **all tests +** or **− L ADT but + L PADT and/or + R PART** → **Pelvis Restoration Program** (maximize hemi 90-90 position)  
- Then when **− PADT** and **3/5 Hruska Abd Lift**: upright L single-leg frontal plane static/dynamic control (left outlet abduction) → upright R single-leg frontal plane (right outlet adduction) → sidelying frontal plane integration → seated left pelvic outlet ascension control → reciprocal alternating activity  

If unable to reposition pelvis: re-evaluate techniques or assessment tests from other PRI courses (manual).

### Left AIC Pelvis Restoration Hierarchy (when − L ADT and + L PADT and/or + R PART)

**Facilitation (Alternating) Program — sequenced:**
1. Turn **on right anterior pelvic inlet via IP ER** (rectus femoris + right sartorius)  
   - 90-90 Supported Left Hip Shift with Right Rectus Femoris and Sartorius  
   - Supine Hooklying Supported Right Rectus Femoris and Sartorius  
   - Supine Hemi Extension with Alternating Respiratory Rectus Femoris and Sartorius (R only)  
2. Turn **on left anterior pelvic outlet via IsP ER** (left puborectalis/pubococcygeus) with left inlet IP IR; turn on left iliococcygeus and left obturator via left adductors  
3. (continues in manual — inhibit right posterior inlet via IP ER to promote right inlet abduction; upright left single-leg frontal plane control; sidelying frontal plane integration; etc.)

**Inhibition (Alternating) Program — sequenced themes from manual:**
1. Inhibition sequencing paired with the above  
2. Inhibit right posterior inlet via IP ER to promote right inlet abduction  
3. Upright left single-leg frontal plane control (left outlet abduction via left iliacus and left …)  
4. Sidelying frontal plane integration (left inlet adduction and right inlet abduction with left …)  

Appendix inhibition families (non-manual titles to know):
- Left Anterior Inlet Inhibition (e.g. 90-90 Respiratory Left Hip …; 90-90 Supported Left Hip Shift with Right Rectus Femoris and Sartorius; Late Left AIC Stance with Right Arm Reach)  
- Right Anterior Outlet Inhibition (Supine Hooklying Adductor …)  
- Right Posterior Inlet Inhibition (All Four Right Glute Max …; Standing Supported Left Knee Flexion with Right Psoas …)  
- Left Posterior Outlet Inhibition (All Four Right Arm Reach …; Standing Supported Left Posterior Outlet Inhibition)  
- Bilateral Anterior Inlet + Bilateral Posterior Outlet Inhibition (Wall and Chair Supported …; Standing Wall Supported …; Squatting Bar Reverse …; Full Functional Squat …; squat & respiration notes)

### PEC Pattern — Pelvis Restoration Hierarchy
Gate: **− Bilateral ADT and (+ …)** per manual hierarchy page. Algorithm page pairs with PEC repositioning techniques and then PEC-specific facilitation/inhibition (abs + posterior pelvic tilt + proximal adductors; bilateral inlet/outlet goals).

### Pathologic PEC Pattern — Hierarchy / Algorithm
Gate: **− Bilateral ADT** with Patho test cluster. Emphasizes pelvic diaphragm / floor isolation considerations (appendix: treatment considerations for isolation of pelvic floor and transverse …). Higher caution; not a "just breathe harder" problem.

### Ilio-Sacral (IS) Instability Treatment Hierarchy for Left AIC patients
Separate ladder — address IS instability sequencing before assuming standard alternating gait integration will stick.

### Left AIC / PEC / Patho PEC repositioning technique lists (appendix)
**Left AIC:** 90-90 Supported Hip Lift with Hemibridge; Right Sidelying Respiratory Left Adductor Pull Back; Left Sidelying Right Glute Max (detailed cues in appendix — also cross-listed under Myokinematic appendix pages in this manual).  

**PEC / Patho PEC (bilateral anterior):** Standing Wall Supported Reach; 90-90 Hip Lift in Passive FA IR with Balloon; Modified All Four Belly Lift.

### Squat & respiration (appendix — Bilateral Anterior Inlet / Posterior Outlet section)
Manual includes dedicated squat ascend/descend respiratory coaching in the pelvis inhibition appendix (pair with Postural Respiration squat-breathing logic). In all variations, squat **ascend** is exhalation-biased to keep ribs down / extensors quiet.

## 6. What NOT to do / common errors

- Do **not** send someone to Myokinematic-only work when **− ADT / + PADT** — that is **Pelvis Restoration**.  
- Do **not** send someone to Pelvis-only work when **− ADT / − PADT** — that is **Myokinematic** (and for bilateral −/−, Myokinematic **+ Postural Respiration**).  
- Do **not** treat PEC as "two Left AICs" without bilateral test logic and bilateral inlet/outlet goals.  
- Do **not** miss Patho PEC: Standing Reach of **0"** with **+ PART** and **Functional Squat > 3/5** is not a win — it is a different bucket.  
- Do **not** skip repositioning into hemi 90-90 neutrality before upright single-leg frontal-plane drills.  
- Do **not** jump to reciprocal alternating activity before inlet/outlet facilitation–inhibition hierarchy and PADT resolution.  
- Do **not** ignore IS instability hierarchy in Left AIC patients who keep losing neutrality.  
- S&C should not improvise manual IP ER / IsP IR / pelvic floor techniques — keep non-manual menus.

Pelvis Restoration sits between raw AF/FA myokinematics and thoraco-abdominal respiration: it owns **whether the pelvic diaphragm and inlet/outlet can alternate** so ZOA and AF IR can stick.

## 8. Achieve programming translation

- Choose breathing/repositioning from the **correct course gate** (PADT/PART vs ADT alone).  
- Left AIC pelvis days: hemibridge / adductor pull-back / right glute max sidelying family → then frontal-plane single-leg control.  
- PEC days: wall-supported reach, 90-90 in passive FA IR with balloon, modified all-four belly lift — bilateral extension bias first.  
- Patho PEC flags → collaborate clinically; don't "progress squat depth" off a 0" reach with + PART.  
- Keep reference centers in coaching: left abs, left ischial seat, left heel, right arch (Integration language shared across intros).
