# Sales discovery questions

Goal: the buyer names their own problem, goal, and cost of waiting before anything is pitched. Ask, pause, listen. Pitch last.

## Achieve rules
- Say "20-minute screen" or "evaluation". Never "complimentary" or "1:1 eval".
- No prices to inbound leads before the evaluation. Evals are in the evening.
- Never invent results or stats. Athlete = talk to the parent too.

## Order
1. Pre-frame intake (before they arrive) 2. Connecting 3. Situation 4. Problem 5. Run the screen, then ask about findings 6. Solution 7. Consequence 8. Qualifying. Then hand off to [Sales eval close](sand-workflow:sales-eval-close).

## 1. Intake form (filled before the eval; read it before talking services)
1. Sport, position, next big date (tryouts, season, showcase, recruiting)?
2. The one thing you most want to improve?
3. Injuries now or in the past year?
4. Training done so far, and what happened with it?
5. If the next 3 months go perfectly, what's different?
6. Why now?
7. Can you commit to 2x a week?
Adults: main goal, current training week, injuries, can you train mornings before work.
Optional repeat-the-pain set: How long have you wanted this? Why so badly? Struggled before? Struggled on your own? Faster with an expert and accountability? Quick or permanent? Will you take our recommendations?

## 2. Connecting (curious tone)
- "What was it that made you want to look into training for [athlete]?"
- "When you saw [the ad / friend's post], what made you want to check it out?"
- "What made now the right time to come in?"
- "What were you hoping to get out of the 20-minute screen?"

## 3. Situation (short)
- "Walk me through what [athlete] does for strength or training right now, in season and off."
- Adults: "What does your week of training look like right now?"

## 4. Problem awareness
- "How do you feel that's going?" / "What's not working the way you'd like?"
- "How long has that been going on?" / "What do you think is causing it?" / "What impact is that having?"
- "How do you mean exactly?" (use any time an answer is vague)

## 5. After the screen (ask, don't tell)
- "Seeing that left-right difference, does that line up with anything you've noticed?"

## 6. Solution awareness
- "If the off-season went exactly the way you wanted, what would be different by spring?"
- "How would that feel for [athlete]?" / Adults: "Six months from now, what would you want to be able to do?"

## 7. Consequence (gently skeptical, only after trust)
- "What happens if things stay how they are going into next season?"
- Accept an honest "probably fine." If so, it may not be a fit now. Never scare parents about injuries.

## 8. Qualifying
- "How important is it to get this started this off-season, as opposed to later?" / "Why is that important to you?"

## Capture for the close
Write down their exact words for: goal, date, problem, feeling, cost of waiting. The recap and transition reuse them verbatim.
