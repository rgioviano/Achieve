# Fitness and Movement programming

This is exercise-program order from PRI Integration for Fitness and Movement (revision September 8, 2020). It is not a Postural Restoration Institute restoration program. Do not coach these lifts as PRI repositioning. Restoration owns 90-90, balloon, adductor pull-back, wall-supported reach, hemi 90-90, and the serratus and low-trap programs. This skill owns how a strength program is ordered once you are programming lifts.

The 2018 manual (revision April 12, 2018) is the same exercise set organized by plane. The 2020 manual adds Screening and the five-step ladder below. No 2018 rule contradicts this skill. Sheet PDFs under Desktop/PRI Techniques/Fitness & Movement are pages from the 2018 manual; **cite the printed title on the sheet, not the filename** (filenames often differ).

The 2020 programming pages do not contain a Patho PEC branch. If they can toe-touch or deep-squat and bilateral ADT and PADT are positive, the Patho PEC skill comes before this five-step ladder.

Page 101 says movement control happens in overlapping waves. The five steps are still printed in order. Do not start at step 5.

## Screens

These monitors do not replace ADT, PADT, PART, standing reach, or the Right BC cluster.

A back squat wants more than 30 degrees of shoulder horizontal abduction to hold the center of mass. Postural Respiration still says horizontal abduction under 45 degrees is a limited test. Thirty is a squat-loading note. Forty-five is a positive-test line. Do not swap them.

- Red: cannot get zones of apposition in three planes. Does not advance to symmetrical loading. Retest in 2 to 4 weeks.
- Yellow: can get a ZOA on both sides but needs help to keep it.
- Green: can inhibit and hold a ZOA on both sides.

Sagittal proximal stability first, then frontal unilateral work, then symmetrical work. Symmetrical presses or overhead only when brachial-chain measures and adduction-lift scores are near symmetrical.

## Five steps

### 1. Shape the left ZOA

Right arm on the exhale. Left ribs down, in, and back.

Short Seated Balloon with Right Arm Reach: sit on an 8 to 12 inch box, knees higher than hips, ball between the knees, left hand on the box, reach the right arm forward while exhaling into the balloon, pause 3 seconds, tongue up, inhale into the back ribs. 4 to 5 breaths. Repeat at least 4 more times.

Squatting Bar Reach: bar inside a rack, fully exhale, round, sit back with heels flat, expand the back ribs, stand on the last exhale through the heels. Arms stay extended. Not an overhead load and not the Patho reverse-reach sheet.

Crab Walk Reach Arounds: left hip back and left AF IR first, then exhale and reach with the right arm, then inhale and reach with the left arm. The left reach is the inhale, after the right-arm exhale.

Standing Wall Supported Resisted Reach is a bilateral serratus punch: reach forward and down on the exhale, low back stays on the wall. The sheet's left-reach-on-inhale option is not a rule that the left arm reaches on the exhale first.

### 2. Left thoracic lateralization

Internally rotate the left ribs on the exhale. Monitor is sidelying shoulder horizontal abduction. Keep the under-45-degree restoration test. Do not turn the monitor into a banded strength day.

Sidelying intercostal inhibition in left AF IR comes after sagittal priming: left side lying, right knee slightly ahead, arc the right arm overhead, inhale into the right upper chest. Not a left-arm overhead press.

If right apical expansion is still clearly worse than the left, go back to Right BC. Left low trap and left serratus before right tricep. Do not program presses to fix that.

### 3. Left pelvis and hip

Left hemipelvis up and back, left hip turns in. Single-leg overhead reach screen, stop at the first failed step. Level 0 is any resting side-shift. Level 2 is both arms overhead without losing the stack. Level 4 is a single-leg squat to 90 degrees in that position. A PEC has a hard time on both sides. Otherwise the adduction screen is harder standing on the left.

### 4. Unilateral, left leg first

Left single-leg RDL before right single-leg RDL. Right hamstrings already team with the right abs and right adductors in a Left AIC. Do not load them until the left ZOA is lateralized and frontally stable. On the right RDL, still pull the left pelvis up with the left adductor.

Left Early Stance with Right Arm Reach: stand on the left, right foot laces-down on a box behind, inhale and reach the right hand down the left shin, exhale and push up through the left heel, left hip stays high, left shoulder stays lower, sets of 8 to 12.

Right Stance with Left Ab Wall only after the left side can do that: exhale up through the right arch, left hip higher than the right, left shoulder lower, right knee stays out.

Do not load right-leg with right-reach just because it feels strong. That is the common ipsilateral PEC-leaning pattern.

### 5. Bilateral only after both legs are grounded

Deadlift proficiency before squat. That sentence is on manual page 111.

Back squat needs more than 30 degrees of shoulder horizontal abduction. Do not tell an athlete with 35 degrees that their Postural Respiration test is negative.

**Squat variety (PRI, F&M 2020 p.117):** a good squatter has variability - goblet, narrow stance, sumo, back, front, Zercher, etc., with the trunk "unlocked" and varying arm placement. Fits Achieve's no-lifetime-bans rule: do not lock one squat forever by pattern.

**Trap Bar Deadlift (PRI, F&M 2018 p.163 / 2020 appendix p.63):** exhale ribs "down and back"; relax the low back to send hips back and down; push with the entire foot (more heel) while scooping the sitz bones under; do not lift the chest or jut the ribcage. Optional variation: rotate the hex bar counterclockwise to assist left grounding, only if the left pelvis stays lateralized and elevated. Sheet does not name handle height - **Achieve floor rule stays: high handles only.**

Squat airflow conflict, keep both: Fitness and Movement page 116 prints inhale down, exhale up as the general programming rule, with an exhale at the top to set the thorax. Thomsen's Pelvis handout prints inhale-down as the earned third variant, and tells heavy lifts to partial-exhale down and finish the exhale up. If they are still a restoration case or red, use Thomsen. If steps 1 to 4 hold and the ZOA holds, the Fitness and Movement rule is the printed programming rule. Do not inhale on the way up.

Symmetrical presses and overhead sit in this same late bucket. If the Right BC gates are failed, go back to that skill. Do not put a soft press here to build serratus.

**Late landmine press options (after steps 1-4 hold; not a shoulder-pain fix):**
- Alternating Thoracic Landmines / Standing Alternating Thoracic Abduction (printed title; file often named Standing Alternating Landmine Press): thorax laterally over the non-pressing arm; exhale press on the right, inhale press on the left; sets of 8-10 (F&M 2018 p.197).
- Left Stance One Arm Landmine / Standing Left AF IR with Right Trunk Rotation (file often Left Stance 1-Arm Landmine Press): left-arm press, left foot behind right; do not extend the left low back; sets of 5-10 (F&M 2018 p.249).

## Hard stops

- Do not file these sheets under restoration.
- Do not start on the right leg, and do not start bilateral work first.
- Do not squat as the first bilateral hinge.
- Do not overhead-press a red athlete or anyone whose apical tests are still asymmetric.
- Do not use the Forward Locomotor Movement lat column as exercise one. It is gait description.
- Do not drop the Patho PEC gate because these five steps never mention it.
- Do not cite F&M sheet filenames as technique names; use the printed title.

## Handoff

Failed ZOA or positive drop tests: leave this skill. Restoration chooses the pattern. Steps 1 to 3 hold and they are not red: unilateral left, then right, then bilateral, deadlift before squat. If bilateral work regresses the tests, the next session starts at step 1.
