# Sales tonality and delivery

## Frame
- Calm, detached expert. Fine either way. Not chasing the sale. Pressure and enthusiasm create resistance.
- Make it about them. Pitch last and short.

## Tones by phase
| Tone | Use |
|---|---|
| Curious | Connecting, situation |
| Confused | Clarifying vague answers and objections ("Think it over?") |
| Playful | Disarming small talk |
| Challenging / skeptical (gentle) | Consequence questions, only after trust |
| Concerned / empathetic | Pain and problem answers |

## Delivery habits
- Slow pace, downward inflection, pause after each question; let silence work.
- Use "feel" not "think" in commitment questions ("Do you feel like this could be the answer?").
- Reuse their exact words in recaps.
- Tone supports honest curiosity, never replaces it. If it sounds scripted, slow down and ask a real follow-up.
- Written messages: follow [Achieve writing style](sand-workflow:achieve-writing-style); no em dashes, no "just following up".
