# Sales offer enhancers (real only)

Core offer design lives in [Hormozi Grand Slam offers](sand-workflow:hormozi-grand-slam-offers). This is the tactical checklist for the sales conversation.

## Rules
- Every claim must be true on the day it is said. No fake counts, dates, or results.
- No prices in ads or first replies; enhancers that involve price are said at the evaluation only.

## Price anchor
Put a higher real number in their head first: regular value of each piece, what private training costs, cost of doing it alone. Then the plan price.

## Splinter stack
Unbundle what you already deliver into named items with values (evaluation, force plate report, custom program, coached sessions, retests, guides). Longer list = more perceived value, hard to compare. Build missing pieces before listing them.

## Scarcity (quantity)
Real limits only: remaining evening slots that fit their schedule, group caps. "Lock it in today and that time is yours."

## Urgency (time)
Real dates: season or tryouts X weeks out, group start dates. "Starting this week gives us X weeks of off-season work."

## Same-day bonus
Achieve: the force plate report is the bonus for signing up at the evaluation. Never give it away in ads. Other bonuses only if they exist.

## Bribe for continuity
High-value item free for joining a membership today; either/or menu of two buying options.

## Downsell your upsell
Lead with the top option and the annual term; if the term blocks, offer the shorter term from the pricing sheet.

## Guarantee
None at Achieve until Ryne sets terms. If he does: match strength to delivery confidence (unconditional, conditional, or "not a fit if").

## Checklist before using any enhancer
- [ ] True today? - [ ] Approved by Ryne? - [ ] Price only said in person at the eval?
