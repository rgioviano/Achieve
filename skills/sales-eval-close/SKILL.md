# Sales eval close (in person, after the 20-minute screen)

Discovery first: [Sales discovery questions](sand-workflow:sales-discovery-questions). Objections: [Sales objection handling](sand-workflow:sales-objection-handling). Offer theory: [Hormozi Grand Slam offers](sand-workflow:hormozi-grand-slam-offers), enhancers: [Sales offer enhancers](sand-workflow:sales-offer-enhancers).

## Rules
- Prices come only from Ryne's current pricing sheet and are said only here, in person. Never in ads or first replies.
- Order: pain and goals, stack, total value, then price.
- Scarcity, urgency, bonuses must be true that day. No invented results. Drafts only; Ryne edits scripts.

## Flow
1. **Recap pain.** "Before I show you anything, here's what you told me and what we saw. You want [goal] before [date]. Right now [screen / force plate finding]. If nothing changes, [cost in their sport, their words]. Did I get that right?" Wait for yes. "Do you want me to show you what I'd do about it?"
2. **Transition (their words).** "Based on what you told me, [logical problem], and that's had [athlete] feeling [emotional problem]. What we'd do is [plan + 1 or 2 parts that answer it]. Does that feel like it might be what you're looking for?" then "Why do you feel like it is?"
3. **Splinter stack (short, only parts that answer their problem; say each item and its value).** Example Build, 2x/week: movement and ROM evaluation (~$149 elsewhere); force plate evaluation and report $59; individual program from today's results $129 to $169; 8 coached sessions a month (private 2x/week is $719 to $829); force plate retests (4 x $59 = $236; monthly for younger athletes); nutrition guide $29 only if it exists. "Altogether that's about $1,321 to $1,471 of value a month." Verify numbers against the current sheet.
4. **Anchor and menu (top option first, annual first).** "Elite ALL-IN is everything we offer, [price] a month on 12 months. Most athletes [athlete]'s age start on Build, the plan I just walked through, at [price] a month on 12 months."
5. **Commitment (feel, not think).** "Do you feel like this could be the answer for [athlete]?" then "Why do you feel it is, though?" then "Really the next step is getting [athlete] set up on [plan] and picking the start week. Would that be appropriate, or how would you like to go from here?" Expect "yes" or "yes, but" (the real concern) and handle it.
6. **Same-day bonus.** "If you start today, today's force plate report is included at no extra charge." (Add other bonuses only if real.)
7. **True scarcity / urgency.** Real remaining evening slots that fit their schedule; real weeks until season or tryouts.
8. **In-season (answer before they ask).** "During your season you can drop to once a week, keep your spot and your strength, and come back without starting over." Cost: bill drops $100/month on Build, $200 on Performance, same term.
9. **30-day guarantee (12-month only).** Say it right after the menu: "I recommend the 12-month. And since you haven't trained with us yet, here's how we make it easy: if you're not happy in the first 30 days, you can cancel with no penalty. The 6-month doesn't come with that." Terms: they must attend every session for their frequency in the first 30 days (8 at 2x/week, 12 at 3x/week); missed sessions made up within the 30 days count. Refund terms are still undecided: either refund month one, or let them cancel with no further charges (recommended).
10. **Downsell.** If the 12-month term is the blocker, offer the shorter term from the pricing sheet before they leave without a plan.

## Avoid
Option closes as pressure tricks ("red or blue") when trust is low; a menu either/or is fine after a yes. Long presentations. Talking price before the recap.

## Open items to confirm with Ryne
Guarantee refund terms; Elite price by term; which shorter Build term is the downsell.
