# Right BC pattern

Restoration layer for the Right Brachial Chain after a left Zone of Apposition is being trained. Not a shoulder workout and not Fitness and Movement.

Bilateral shoulder horizontal abduction under 45 degrees both sides can be a bilateral BC and can also be a bilateral PEC. If bilateral ADT and PADT are positive, PEC or Patho PEC owns the first block. Do not add a mirrored left BC program.

## Best set, in this order

Pelvis and left zone of apposition first. Then this list. If apical expansion is still asymmetric, stop before right tricep and subscapularis.

1. 90-90 hip lift with right arm reach and balloon.
2. PRI wall supported squat with balloon. Right hand across midline on the exhale.
3. Left sidelying knee toward knee with balloon.
4. Right sidelying right apical expansion with left arm reach and left adductor. Houglum's paired drill for Left AIC plus Right BC. Press the left knee down before the left arm reaches. Do not keep cueing right apical expansion if the left abs are not pushing the left hip into the surface, the right glute is off, or the right knee is not forward (Houglum consult, 2024-06-02).
5. Standing supported passive left AF IR with right trunk rotation.

If the right apical test is still behind the left: left low trap and left serratus before right low trap and right tricep.

If the five-test cluster is already clear: supine right tricep, then the turn of the right hand toward the mat (subscapularis, not lat). The rest of the Right Subscapularis folder (supine, sidelying, seated, prone, then standing) comes after that, not before. Sidelying three-point stance is later and the sheet does not name a side. Do not guess one.

Positive humeral IR on the test means the scapula is sitting in protraction. Deflate and get internal rotation first, then air into the lateral rib cage. Right scapular internal rotation also needs left posterior mediastinum expansion (Houglum consult). Do not start with lat or pec as the internal-rotation muscle.

## Who this pattern is

Track the Right BC cluster. Do not invent cutoffs.

- Left shoulder horizontal abduction under 45 degrees.
- Left cervical axial rotation limited. No degree cutoff is printed.
- Left humeral-glenoid flexion limited. No degree cutoff is printed.
- Right apical expansion limited compared with the left. Negative right apical with positive left apical after a Superior T4 attempt is a clinician subclavius path, not a gym drill.
- Right HG IR under 80 to 90 degrees is limited. The overview routes to right subscapularis when the other tests are still positive but right HG IR is 80 degrees or better.

If they can toe-touch or deep-squat and both ADT and PADT are positive, Patho PEC is first.

## Two orders

Locked gate: if apical expansion is still asymmetric, left low trap and left serratus before right low trap and right tricep.

The Postural Respiration non-manual CD prints the reverse default and starts the right-trap ladder supine. Use the CD order only when all five cluster tests are already negative. Still start supine.

## ZOA entry scripts

1. 90-90 Hip Lift with Right Arm Reach and Balloon. Script is in Left AIC. Right arm to the ceiling on the exhale.
2. PRI Wall Supported Squat with Balloon. Heels 7 to 10 inches from the wall. Ball between the knees. Balloon in the left hand. Sit hips to the wall, round the back, shift the left hip back. Exhale and reach the right hand forward and across midline. Four breaths. Repeat 4 more times.
3. Left Sidelying Knee Toward Knee with Balloon. Press the left hip down. Shift the right knee forward. Turn the right knee out. Turn the left knee up with the left inner thigh. Blow the balloon 4 breaths.
4. Right Sidelying Right Apical Expansion with Left Arm Reach and Left Adductor. Press the left knee into the bolster before the left arm reaches.
5. Standing Supported Passive Left AF IR with Right Trunk Rotation. Script is in Left AIC. Left hip back first.

## Hard stops

- No cuff, true overhead, or ER-into-overhead while rib position and right apical expansion are unfinished.
- No right low trap or right tricep while apical expansion is still asymmetric.
- No left reach as the first reach of the day.
- No lat pulldown as the IR solution.
- No Superior T4 or subclavius manual technique in the gym.

## Handoff

Cluster clears and reach is clean in supine, prone, and left sidelying: standing right low trap and right tricep, still not a loaded press. Pelvis tests regress: return to Left AIC. Lift order is Fitness and Movement, only after this gate.
