# Achieve Reactivate win-backs

Standing assignment locked 2026-09-09 (timing floor locked same day). Sales drafts; PA finds and schedules; nothing sends until Ryne says send on that exact draft.

## Hand-off
1. PA finds people who stopped training.
2. PA labels the last thread `Reactivate` and sets a suggested reach date from **why they left**.
3. Each morning (if any due), PA hands Sales the due rows.
4. Sales drafts reactivation emails and posts them in **Administrative** (or to CoS).

## Timing (locked 2026-09-09)
- **Cancels / quits / ETF:** wait **at least 2 months** from the cancel decision date before any win-back. If PA hands a cancel that is not yet 2 months out, **do not draft** (or hold until the floor date).
- **After the 2-month floor, context wins.** Example: left for high school / in-season → do not ping mid-season even if the floor is met; wait until offseason or a natural end (e.g. ~Nov for fall HS). Shape the reach date and the draft from leave reason, not calendar math alone.
- **Soft pauses with a restart date are separate** from cancels. If the restart date passed with no return, they can be due without the 2-month cancel floor - still confirm they are not already training before drafting/sending.
- **Skip** people who left angry (no win-back).

## Draft rules
- Run [Pre-draft check](sand-workflow:pre-draft-check) on each person first: name plus phone or email match, no Gmail/Quo touch in the last 48h, any offered time in the future and open, parent only if confirmed, no prices unless read live today. Also confirm they are not already training again (Mindbody or sheet).
- Voice: `achieve-writing-style` / `achieve-mail`.
- Tone: [3-7-30](sand-workflow:sales-nurture-3-7-30) value sequence - useful even if they never come back. Not “just buy.”
- Shape the ask and timing from the leave reason.
- Signature: plain-text CSCS block, **no** website line (Gmail tracking).
- Downsell/keep patterns still apply if relevant (1x/week, pause) - see Mozi + cancel locks.

## Out of scope
SCDD Instant Form first text and SCDD E1–E4 stay product locks. Do not rewrite those as Reactivate.
