# Lead contact and follow-up

Operating rules from speed-to-lead research (MIT/InsideSales, HBR, Velocify, XANT, VanillaSoft–Telfer) plus SMS reminder evidence. Pair with brand voice and product locks when drafting. Drafts only until the owner reviews. Never invent prices, proof, or availability.

## Mindset

- Speed-to-lead is the highest-leverage variable for inbound web leads.
- Value sequence beats “just checking in.” Earn each ask.
- Multi-channel beats phone-only or email-only.
- Persistence has a ceiling: past ~7–8 zero-engagement attempts, stop escalating.

## Initial contact (Day 0)

1. **Auto SMS and/or email in seconds:** we received the request, who we are, next step. Marketing SMS needs prior consent (TCPA); identify sender; include STOP when required.
2. **Human phone within 1–5 minutes** during open hours. If staffing cannot hit that, still aim under 30–60 minutes. Do not wait overnight on fresh inbound.
3. **Order:** SMS ack → phone → short voicemail if no answer → same-day email (what to expect + book path).
4. **First live call:** name + business, why calling, one goal/timing question, soft next step (book the intro/screen). No price dump. No hard close on the first ring.
5. **Voicemail:** name, reason, callback number. No full pitch.

## Attempt caps

| Channel | Cap / guidance |
|---|---|
| Phone to first contact | ~6 attempts (most converters reached by call 6; more often hurts) |
| Multi-channel active sequence | ~2–7 attempts over ~6–8 days |
| Email before first contact | ~5 in the first month (more than 5 can hurt) |
| Hard stop escalate | ~7–8 multi-channel touches with zero engagement |

Ignore weak viral claims like “80% of sales need 5+ follow-ups.” Prefer the tables above.

## Active cadence (inbound high-intent)

| When | Touches |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Auto SMS/email → call → VM + SMS → second call → email → optional third call |
| Day 2 | SMS or call |
| Day 3 | Call + one value email |
| Day 5 | Call + SMS |
| Day 7 | Soft close-loop (still looking / still want X?) |
| Days 8–14 | Value touches + phone push around day 14–15 |
| Days 22–30 | Value email + breakup / permission |
| 30+ | Monthly nurture or archive until new intent |

Space active contact attempts about **1–2 days** apart after Day 0. Front-load the first week.

## Longer value nurture (when not same-day chase)

Use a value sequence (e.g. 3-7-30 style) when product copy is not already locked:

- **Days 1–3:** best case study → best transformation → biggest insight
- **Next ~7 days:** one touch every other day; each removes **one** objection with proof
- **Next ~30 days:** ~two touches/week; alternate proof and insight; each touch stands alone even if they never buy

Product-locked sequences always win over this template for those specific sends.

## Channel rules

1. High-intent inbound: phone early for the live conversation.
2. Interleave email with calls (email between dials improves reach).
3. Use at least two channels.
4. SMS strengths: instant ack, confirmations, reminders. Not a substitute for the qualify/book call.
5. Quiet hours and consent for SMS. Opt-out stops everything.

## Message themes by touch

| Touch | Say | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Auto SMS/email | Received; who we are; next step | Pitch, discount dump |
| First live call | Confirm interest; goal + timeline; offer intro/screen | Hard close on price |
| Voicemail | Name, reason, callback # | Full pitch |
| Same-day retries | Tried you; 2 time options | Guilt |
| Mid-sequence | What intro includes; proof; one FAQ | Fake urgency |
| Breakup | Permission to close or nudge later | Passive-aggressive |
| Booked | Confirm time/place/expect/reschedule | Upsell before show |
| Reminders | 24h + 2–3h: details + YES/reschedule | Changing the deal |
| No-show | Life happens + rebook | Shame |

## When to stop

- Opt-out / STOP / clear no → stop immediately
- ~6 unanswered calls → cut phone; nurture or pause
- ~7–8 multi-channel with zero engagement → stop escalating
- After breakup with no reply → monthly nurture or archive
- Invalid number → scrub
- Do not keep hammering after a failed Day-0 burst past ~20 hours

## Show rate (booked appointments)

1. Instant confirm (SMS + email)
2. Reminder at ~24 hours and ~2–3 hours before
3. Multiple notifications beat a single one
4. No-show: rebook within ~30–60 minutes when possible
5. Kill/optimize on show and booked→enroll, not vanity CPL alone

## Draft checklist

Before sending a draft pack:

- [ ] Speed path clear (auto ack + human call timing)
- [ ] Channel order and attempt caps respected
- [ ] Cadence spaced (not 10 emails Day 0)
- [ ] Each touch has a theme; value or clear next step
- [ ] Stop / breakup defined
- [ ] Booked leads get confirm + dual reminders
- [ ] Brand voice + product locks applied; no invented prices/proof
- [ ] [Pre-draft check](sand-workflow:pre-draft-check) passed for every recipient: name plus phone or email match the sheet, no Gmail/Quo touch in the last 48h that changes it, offered times in the future and open on the calendar, "parent" only if confirmed, no prices unless read live today
