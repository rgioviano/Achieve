# Sales follow-up after eval (no-sign)

Pre-booking chase lives in [Lead contact and follow-up](sand-workflow:lead-contact-and-follow-up) and the speed-to-book sequence; value nurture in [Sales nurture 3-7-30](sand-workflow:sales-nurture-3-7-30); lapsed members in [Achieve Reactivate win-backs](sand-workflow:achieve-reactivate-win-backs). Voice: [Achieve writing style](sand-workflow:achieve-writing-style).

## Rules
- Drafts only. Nothing sends without Ryne's approval of the final text and recipient.
- No em dashes. No "just following up". No invented results. No prices unless already discussed at their eval.
- Use "evaluation" or "20-minute screen"; next steps are in the evening.
- Athletes: write to the parent only when the sheet or one of their messages confirms the contact is a parent; otherwise write neutrally.
- Run [Pre-draft check](sand-workflow:pre-draft-check) before showing any draft (2-field recipient match, last-48h Gmail/Quo touches, future open times, no prices unless read live today).

## Steps
1. Pull their eval notes: goal, date, problem, feeling, the objection they gave, who else decides.
2. Match the objection:
   - Spouse: offer a short time that week with both parents.
   - Season: restate the in-season once-a-week option and the date the off-season starts.
   - Think it over / price: one question back to their own goal.
   - Waiting on PT or logistics: soft, no pressure, offer the next step when they're ready.
3. Core line (their words): "You mentioned [their goal]. Do you still feel like getting started before [deadline] makes sense for [athlete]?"
4. End with one easy next step (two real times, or "reply with a couple of evenings").
5. Cadence: a few days after the eval, then about 7, 14, 30 days with value (a relevant insight or real, consented proof). Close the loop around day 45 ("I'll stop reaching out... just text me here"). Stop on any clear no or opt-out.
6. Real proof only: named athletes need their or their parents' OK.
