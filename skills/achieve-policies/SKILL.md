# Achieve policies and pricing

Source: Ryne Gioviano. Policies 2026-10-12, in-season rule 2026-10-09, pricing sheet 2026-10-22 (achieve-pricing-2026-10.pdf in this folder). This is the source of truth. When Ryne changes something, rewrite that line and add a changelog entry.

## Policies
- Late cancel: cancellations must come more than 24 hours before the session start (spots are held in limited-capacity sessions). Cancels with less than 24 hours' notice, and no-shows, are charged for that session after one courtesy pass (Ryne confirmed the courtesy pass still applies, 2026-10-31; 24-hour rule restated 2026-10-26).
- Self-booking closes 12 hours before a session.
- Cancelling mid-term: 30% of the remaining balance plus $25.
- Stopping a debit: 5 days' notice. (Account-change processing of at least 3 days, Ryne 2026-10-26, may be separate; confirm with Chief if unclear.)
- Account changes: bots do not make them. Refer the person to a human and say Achieve needs at least 3 days to process any change (Ryne, 2026-10-26).
- Package windows: 4 weeks for adults, 5 weeks for athletes.
- Minimum age 11. Minimum commitment 3 months.
- First eval is free. Never call it complimentary or 1:1.
- Anyone off more than a month returns at the current rate.
- Athletes in season: 12-month term; may optionally drop to 1x/week in season and resume after. A step-down, not a pause.
- Annual (12-month) contracts can't be paused (Ryne, 2026-10-23). Offer a lighter or modified program instead.
- Pause length (Ryne, 2026-10-23): a pause is generally allowed for 1 month at most, and the client keeps their grandfathered rate. The one exception is an in-season hold, where the restart date can be up to 2 weeks after the high school season ends (for example, May 1 after state). Anyone who comes back after a longer break restarts at the current rate. When confirming a pause, give the restart-by date.
- Second family member: 15% off. Mention it only when they ask or it will likely help close the sale, never by default (Ryne, 2026-10-23).
- All services continue until written notice is received. Clients pre-pay monthly. No setup fee on any tier.

## Pricing (per month: 3 mo / 6 mo / 12 mo)
### Athletes, small group
Billing recurs when all sessions for the month are used or five weeks pass, whichever comes first.
- Foundation 1x/wk: $229 / $209 / $189
- Build 2x/wk: $329 / $309 (most popular) / $289
- Performance 3-4x/wk: $429 / $409 / $389
- Elite 3-4x/wk all-in: $629 / $599 / $569. Includes 1 massage/mo, 4 Normatec/mo, 1 nutrition consult/qtr, 1 re-eval/qtr, pre-hab and mobility plan.

### Adults, small group
Billing recurs every four weeks.
- Essential 1x/wk: $279 / $239 / $199
- Active 2x/wk: $379 / $339 / $299
- Committed 3-4x/wk: $479 / $439 / $399 (12 mo most popular)

### Private 1-on-1
Per athlete. Sessions don't roll over. Billed every four weeks.
- Essential 1x/wk: $439 / $409 / $389
- Active 2x/wk: $829 / $769 / $719
- Committed 3x/wk: $1,149 / $1,069 / $999

### Additional services
- Remote training (app; includes in-person or remote eval): $169 / $149 / $129
- Hybrid remote add-on to an in-person package: 1 day/wk +$59, 2 days +$79, 3 days +$99 per month
- Sports massage: 30 min $55, 60 min $85, 5x30 min $250, 5x60 min $400
- Normatec: 30 min $30, 45 min $45
- Nutrition consult: $69 per session
- Add-on packages must be used within 6 months of purchase.

## How to use in messages
1. Answer what they asked first. Bring up a policy only when it affects their request.
2. Cancel or hold requests: lead with a way to keep training (modified program, 1x/week, in-season step-down), then offer cancel or pause at the end of the billing period. Never lead with fees; mention 30% plus $25 only if they push to cancel mid-term.
3. New leads and inquiries: lead emails may include ONE line like "Pricing starts at $X/mo." with X read live from this sheet that day (Ryne, 2026-10-23). No other prices to leads, and none in ads. Run pre_draft_check with --first-touch --price-source <this sheet>. Answer the question, then book the eval, where full value and pricing are covered.
4. Existing clients asking about plans, switches or costs: quote from this sheet. If Mindbody or Ryne shows a different number for that person (older rate, special deal), use theirs and flag the mismatch to Ryne.
5. Youth under 11: say politely that the minimum age is 11.
6. Still run the pre-draft check and writing-style rules.
