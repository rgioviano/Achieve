# Patho PEC pattern (restoration)

Own treatment path. Pelvis Restoration prints a separate algorithm and hierarchy titled Pathologic PEC. Not a footnote inside PEC.

Same bilateral extension bias as PEC. Diaphragm description changes: PEC is descended, hypertonic, and inflexible. Patho PEC is descended, bulged, and high resting tone. PEC cannot touch the toes, PART is negative, squat is under 3/5. Patho PEC can touch the toes or full-squat, PART is positive on one or both sides, squat is over 3/5, and ADT and PADT stay positive both sides. The reach looks better. That is why you are here.

The first three repositioning titles match PEC. The hierarchy does not. Step 1 is the passive-FA-IR balloon. Step 2 is inlet extension and posterior expansion. Step 3 is the first legal Left AIC facilitation.

## Best set, in this order

This order is the Pathologic PEC hierarchy in Pelvis Restoration (2024, page 85), not a menu. Do not open Left AIC facilitation because the toe-touch looks good.

1. Supine 90-90 hip lift in passive FA IR with balloon. If there is time for one drill, this is it.
2. Wall supported passive FA IR with balloon.
3. Wall and chair supported IO/TA.
4. Standing wall supported reach. Both arms forward and down.
5. Squatting bar reverse reach with a pole. One sheet. The manual calls it levels 2-3. The sheet's own goal is PRI squat level four or five. Do not substitute the Fitness and Movement bar reach.
6. Modified all-four belly lift.
7. All-four belly lift, the non-modified sheet (knees off, legs straight, as printed).
8. Seated bar reach.

Left AIC alternating work is step 3 of that hierarchy, and only after a Hruska abduction lift of at least 1/5. Hemi 90-90 is for a leftover frontal-plane limiter, not a replacement for steps 1 to 8.

## Who this pattern is

All of these:

- Can toe-touch (standing reach 0 inches) or can full-squat, and ADT and PADT are positive.
- ADT positive both sides.
- PADT positive both sides.
- Matrix also prints PART positive on one or both sides, functional squat greater than 3/5, Hruska Abduction Lift under 3/5.

Level 3 is bottom below the knees, heels down, trunk flexed. Level 4 is bottom to the heels. Level 5 is a maximal heel-centered squat. Above 3/5 with the drop tests still positive is patho, not mobility to load.

Left AIC usually cannot touch the toes. Do not give a patho athlete the hemibridge and glute-med menu first.

## Step 1 script

Feet on the wall, hips and knees at 90, feet wider than the hips. 4 to 6 inch ball between the knees. Exhale into a pelvic tilt, low back flat. Blow the balloon, pause 3 seconds, tongue on the roof of the mouth. Inhale and relax the tilt. Blow and redo the tilt. 4 to 5 breaths. Repeat 4 more times.

Cheat: marching the right leg off the wall. That is the Left AIC hemibridge. Passive FA IR means the feet are wide. It does not mean cranking the femurs in with the lats.

## Step 2 scripts you can run

Standing Wall Supported Reach: heels 7 to 10 inches from the wall, ball between the knees, low back flat, both arms reach forward and down on the exhale, inhale into the upper back, 4 to 5 breaths, repeat 4 more times. Bilateral forward reach. Not overhead.

Modified All Four Belly Lift: round, tuck, weight forward, lift one hand without twisting, 4 to 5 breaths each side.

The other step-2 names in the best-set list are the 2012 pelvis sheets (wall passive FA IR balloon, wall and chair IO/TA, squatting bar reverse reach, non-modified all-four belly lift, seated bar reach). Run them in that list order. Do not relabel a Fitness and Movement squatting bar reach as the reverse reach.

## Hard stops

- No hemibridge marches, left glute-med emphasis, or retro stairs as the opening block.
- No celebration of toe-touch or deep squat while ADT and PADT are positive.
- No left reach as the patho reach. Bilateral forward reach is the patho reach.
- No right tricep, cuff, true overhead, or ER-to-overhead while apical expansion is still the bilateral cluster.
- No loaded squat or deadlift to use the range they already have.
- No pelvic-floor techniques and no PRI Kegel until tests are negative and the abduction lift is 4/5.

## Handoff

Bilateral ADT and PADT negative: Myokinematic and Postural Respiration. PADT still positive: stay in step 2. Left PADT at a 1/5 abduction lift: Left AIC skill. Still stuck after an honest step 1 and 2: refer. Do not add volume. Gym lift order is Fitness and Movement, and not while they are still a patho screen.
