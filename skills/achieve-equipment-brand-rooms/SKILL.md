# Achieve equipment vs brand-room constraints

Never invent missing gear. Never bleed one brand-room's limits onto another.

## Two rooms

1. **Achieve (Aurora facility)** — full gym programming default. Has dipping belts; use them for weighted chin-ups. Trap bar, landmine, cables, racks, Hawkin plates, and the usual Achieve house-equipment language from Ryne's TrainHeroic style apply here unless he says otherwise.
2. **NTA dryland (New Trier Aquatics weight room)** — thin equipment only. Locked constraints: Monday Day 1 first ~30 min is hallway/stairwell (adult class owns the gym); no racks in short course until April; no TRX; ~2 kettlebells, ~2 cables; **no SSB**; **no dipping belts**. Single-limb reps are per side. Notes are for coaches.

Achieve ≠ NTA. The no-dipping-belts / thin-equipment rule is **NTA only**. Do not apply it to Achieve athlete or adult programs.

## Before you constrain a program

1. Name the room: Achieve Aurora, NTA dryland, remote, or another site he named.
2. Use only equipment known for that room (this skill, Achieve PM memory, or what Ryne just said).
3. If gear is unknown, ask once — do not invent "we don't have X" or "use DB between ankles because no belt."
4. For Achieve chin-up loading, default to dipping belt unless he says that athlete cannot use it.

## Anti-patterns

- Claiming Achieve has no dipping belts
- Copying NTA hallway/stairwell or rack limits into an Achieve block
- Inventing missing cables, belts, SSB, or plates to force a regression
- Mixing IronBlueprint product constraints into gym programming
