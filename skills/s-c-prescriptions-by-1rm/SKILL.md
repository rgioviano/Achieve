# S&C prescriptions by %1RM

Full source: `/home/box/agent-data/shared/sc-reference/sc-prescriptions-pct1rm.txt`

## Levels
- Beginner: <6 months - prioritize technique, start lower %1RM
- Intermediate: 6–18 months consistent
- Advanced: long training age (header says “18+ years”; treat as advanced relative to house beg/int/adv, not literal decades)

## Goals (rough load bands)
- Power velocity: ~30–60% · Power force: ~70–90%
- Strength: >80–85%
- Hypertrophy: ~60–85%
- GPP: ~40–60%

## Phases
- Offseason: higher volume, moderate-lower %1RM
- Preseason: %1RM up, volume down
- In-season: lower volume, sustain %1RM
- Taper/peaking: volume −40–60%, high %1RM, 1–2 weeks

Open the full tables in the source file and pick **specific** values (not ranges) for the athlete’s level × goal × phase. Prefer TrainHeroic house naming and blank load lines when writing Achieve menus.
