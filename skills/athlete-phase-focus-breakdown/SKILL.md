# Athlete phase focus breakdown

Full source: `/home/box/agent-data/shared/sc-reference/athlete-phase-focus-breakdown.txt`

| Phase | Focus | Typical loading | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Off-season | Hypertrophy / rebuild | 8-12 reps, ~60-80% 1RM, RPE 6-8 | 1-2 wk after season; ~8-12 wk |
| Preseason | Strength | 4-6 reps, ~70-85% 1RM, RPE 7-9 | ~6-8 wk out; lasts ~6-8 wk |
| In-season | Strength + power maintain | Power 2-5 @ ~50-70% fast; strength ~70-85% low volume | Competitive season |
| Taper / peaking | Power, volume cut 40-60% | 2-4 reps, keep intensity | 2-3 wk before major event |

Open the source for full example weeks (SMR through tertiary). Pair with [Guide to tapering](sand-workflow:guide-to-tapering) and [S&C prescriptions by %1RM](sand-workflow:s-c-prescriptions-by-1rm). Hawkin limiter still wins for exercise choice inside the phase.
