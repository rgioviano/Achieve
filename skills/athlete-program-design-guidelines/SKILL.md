# Athlete program design guidelines

Full source: `/home/box/agent-data/shared/sc-reference/athlete-program-design-guidelines.md`

## Always
- Customize from goals, assessment, training age, chronological age, sport/position/stroke, and season phase.
- Target ~1 hour. Cook before chef - don’t invent a new format.
- Neurological order (most → least demanding):
  1. SMR (3-5 areas from eval/sport)
  2. Breathing (1-2)
  3. Warm-up (5-6 movement prep + exactly 2 dynamics: 1 lower, 1 upper)
  4. Speed/agility when used (1 exercise, max intent, full recovery) - fresh, after warm-up
  5. Power (typically 2: 1 lower + 1 upper) before strength
  6. Strength: 2 primary (heavy compounds) + 2 secondary (lighter/quality)
  7. Core, then tertiary/accessory
- Primary = heavily loaded multi-joint (trap-bar, front squat, chin-up, etc.). Secondary = cables, unilateral balance, bodyweight, isolation.
- Non-competing pairs: knee-dominant with pull; hip-dominant with push.
- Overhead athletes: landmine / horizontal press over true vertical press.
- Programming-room drafts only unless Ryne says to write TrainHeroic. Hawkin first when force plates matter.
