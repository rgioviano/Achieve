## Discounts (locked 2026-10-05, updated 2026-10-23)
- **Referral:** a referring client gets 20% off their next month when the person they referred signs up.
- **Siblings / second family member:** 15% off every month for as long as both keep training.
- Only offer the 15% when the family asks or when it will likely help close the sale. Bots do not bring it up otherwise.
- Discounts never stack. Both discounts include adults.
- All bots quote these terms the same way.

## Pauses and holds (Ryne, 2026-10-23)
- Pauses are capped at 1 month and keep the grandfathered rate.
