- (2026-09-05) Early Peak Bimodal (Achieve CMJ guide): high braking but inefficient braking→propulsion transfer / amortization lag. Prescribe ballistic isometrics at peak-braking position, band-assisted/unweighted rapid SSC (≈10–15% BW assist), continuous force through transition; avoid lengthening the brake or long-ecc squats when unweight is already long.
- (2026-09-05) EUR (CMJ/SJ height): McGuigan et al. 2006 (JSCR) introduced EUR as SSC/phase-sensitive; values >1 common. Later work (e.g. Appl Mech 2022 reviews; Kozinc 2024) cautions EUR alone is not a training prescription-high EUR can reflect weak SJ/RFD/muscle slack. Always pair EUR with phase times and curve shape. EUR ≈1.0 (CMJ≈SJ) → rebuild SSC/faster drop; EUR ≥~1.1 with slow unweight → keep SSC, attack unweight + transition (not “more eccentric”).
- (2026-09-05) Overhead athletes: last three exercises = 2 core + 1 shoulder-health (Ryne 2026-09-05). Use Achieve house core pairing rules and shoulder-health progressions from Strength Coach docs - Research does not invent those pairings.
- (2026-09-05) Swinton et al. 2011 (JSCR): hexagonal/trap-bar deadlift vs straight bar - lower peak lumbar, hip, and ankle moments; higher knee moment; often higher peak power. Prefer trap bar (or front-loaded/goblet patterns) when bar axial LBP is the complaint and plates don’t explain the back.
- (2026-09-08) Squat depth limiters (Kim et al. 2015, J Hum Kinet): ankle dorsiflexion ROM is primary in both sexes; males also hip flexion ROM; females also ankle dorsiflexor strength. Limited DF → less knee flexion, more trunk lean/compensations (Malliaras/Macrum-type findings). Screen weight-bearing lunge DF first; heel elevation (~25 mm) can bypass DF limit while training depth.
- (2026-09-08) Deep vs shallow heavy squats (Bloomquist et al. 2013, Eur J Appl Physiol): 12 wk deep squat (to ~120° knee) vs shallow (~60°) - deep group superior front-thigh CSA and isometric strength at deeper angles; shallow gains transferred poorly to deep. To build depth under load: train the deepest controllable ROM with progressive heavy low-rep sets (not high-rep pump). Pause at bottom and/or box-to-depth owns the position without high reps.
- (2026-09-08) Early Peak Plateau (Achieve CMJ guide): force at transition already ~95% of peak and stays near-peak early; limiter is transfer/amortization (not the drop). Prefer Split Squat ISO Push (~quarter-squat, overcoming/ballistic) at peak-braking position + rapid initiation/assisted SSC quality (“hold the plateau and fire”); mid-thigh iso sits too high and misses the hang. Avoid lengthening the brake or long-ecc. Differs from Early Peak Bimodal (which favors bounce-through).
- (2026-09-08) Swim dryland upper/lower balance (Research synthesis + SC/PM/Hawkin consensus): Default keep both lowers across the week (one knee-dom day, one hip-dom day) with vertical + horizontal pull distributed across days.
