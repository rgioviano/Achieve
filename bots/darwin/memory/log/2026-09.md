- (2026-09-05) EUR (CMJ/SJ height): McGuigan et al. 2006 (JSCR) introduced EUR as SSC/phase-sensitive; values >1 common. Later work (e.g. Appl Mech 2022 reviews; Kozinc 2024) cautions EUR alone is not a training prescription—high EUR can reflect weak SJ/RFD/muscle slack. Always pair EUR with phase times and curve shape. EUR ≈1.0 (CMJ≈SJ) → rebuild SSC/faster drop; EUR ≥~1.1 with slow unweight → keep SSC, attack unweight + transition (not “more eccentric”).

- (2026-09-08) Squat depth limiters (Kim et al. 2015, J Hum Kinet): ankle dorsiflexion ROM is primary in both sexes; males also hip flexion ROM; females also ankle dorsiflexor strength. Limited DF → less knee flexion, more trunk lean/compensations (Malliaras/Macrum-type findings). Screen weight-bearing lunge DF first; heel elevation (~25 mm) can bypass DF limit while training depth.
- (2026-09-08) Deep vs shallow heavy squats (Bloomquist et al. 2013, Eur J Appl Physiol): 12 wk deep squat (to ~120° knee) vs shallow (~60°) — deep group superior front-thigh CSA and isometric strength at deeper angles; shallow gains transferred poorly to deep. To build depth under load: train the deepest controllable ROM with progressive heavy low-rep sets (not high-rep pump). Pause at bottom and/or box-to-depth owns the position without high reps.
