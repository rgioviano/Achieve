# Cressey baseball implementables for Achieve wraps (from Scout, 2026-10-05)
Full sourced brief: cressey-baseball-brief-full-2026-10-05.md (EC vs CSP-staff labeled). Drafts only; not locked by Ryne.

## Gate first (every thrower)
- Shoulder profile: total IR+ER arc, Beighton laxity, scap resting position -> tight / loose / in-between. Tight = mobility; loose = stabilize, skip stretching (rhythmic stabs, standing activation).
- Workload: innings/pitches, two-way/catcher/SS, months fully off throwing. Upper volume, med ball, arm care all drop as throwing rises.
- Cross-check with PRI (L AIC / R BC, PEC/Patho) before any overhead or cross-body call.

## Strength Coach use
- Lifting menu: DL variations, front/SSB squat, single-leg, rows, chin-ups (vertical pull <=1x/wk in-season), push-ups, DB/neutral press, landmine or bottoms-up KB overhead. Avoid straight-bar bench, upright rows, front/side raises, Oly lifts, heavy OH press.
- Cuff: strength + timing (manual ER, rhythmic stabs, 90/90, ball-release holds). Small daily doses, never to failure, never heavy right before throwing. Good tertiary slot in trisets.
- Scap: cue upward rotation as arm elevates; not everything "down and back." Watch lat takeover / arm drift on ER drills.
- T-spine mobility without arm reach (early off-season + in-season); windmills months 3-4. No KB arm bar.
- Med ball: 5-step rotational progression; ages 13-15 stay steps 1-2; 4-8 lb; cues head behind belly button, slingshot legs, hand to wall. In-season low volume, mostly opposite throwing side.
- Post-outing (within 24 h): restore IR and elbow extension; side-lying cross-body only if screen calls for it; no flush runs.
- In-season: starters on 7-day template; relievers Mon/Thu lower volume; position players ~2 full-body/wk; ~10-14 total sets, 35-40 min (fits our 35-45 rule).
- Conditioning: <=60 yd sprints at 90%+, 2x/wk (3 max). No distance running (>150 m).

## Conflicts / not ours
- LOCKED (Ryne 2026-10-05): baseball in-season, primaries carry over; secondary/tertiary rotate only to close variations (same pattern, similar loading), no brand-new movements.
- Long toss, weighted balls, throwing programs, 2+ months off throwing, inning caps, showcase advice = coach/parent education only; Achieve is gym-only and does not program throwing unless Ryne asks.
- Changed views (sleeper stretch, overhead lifting, cuff frequency, early off-season power) are dated in the full brief; use latest.
