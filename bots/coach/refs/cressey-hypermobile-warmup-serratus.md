# Cressey Sports Performance — Hypermobile Warm-up & Serratus Sequencing

**Source:** Eric Cressey / CSP case studies (Ryne, 2026-10-02). Keep reasoning with each exercise when wrapping.

## Sample Hypermobile Client Warm-up

1. **Supine Hooklying KB Pullover** ×10 — Lats + core so arms go overhead without rib flare or low-back arch.
2. **Side Bridge w/ Bottoms-Up KB Screwdriver** ×6/side/dir — Side-core + shoulder stability; cuff keeps joint centered under controlled rotation.
3. **Supine Bridge March** ×10/side — Glutes drive hip; core keeps pelvis from moving.
4. **Lateral Bear Crawls** ×10/side — Load shoulders/hips while moving; muscular stability loose joints lack from ligaments.
5. **Honest Hip CARs** ×6/side — Muscles control full hip ROM so the joint isn’t getting banged up.
6. **Serratus Wall Slide w/ Roller** ×10 — Serratus guides scapula so upper traps/ribs don’t take over into OH.
7. **Wall Slides w/ Upward Rotation & Lift-off** ×10 — Lower trap + cuff at top of OH, where loose shoulders have least passive support.
8. **Glute Wall March Iso Holds** ×(3×5s/side) — Glute/core isometrics hold pelvis stable instead of more range they already have.
9. **1-Leg RDL KB Switches** ×10/side — Single-leg glute/hamstring; soft knee (don’t lock out); KB orbit around stable leg builds stability.
10. **DB Goblet Split-Squat Iso Holds** ×15–20s/side — Load glutes/quads at bottom so muscles, not joints, hold the position.
11. **Double Clutch Overhead Med-Ball Stomp to Floor** ×6 (4–8 lb, individual) — Lats/core/cuff control OH and absorb force at speed.

## Serratus Sequencing

Progress from positioning/breathing/mobility to inhibit antagonists (scalenes, lats), then isolated → integrated serratus challenges:

1. All Four Belly Lift — 5 breaths
2. Side-Lying Rib Roll — 8/side
3. Hip Shift w/ Lat Stretch — 5 breaths/side
4. 1-arm Serratus Wall Slides w/ Valslides & Contralateral Thoracic Rotation — 8/side
5. Split Squat w/ Banded Reach — 8/side
6. Thoracic Bridge — 5/side
7. Knee Hug to Lateral Lunge w/ Overhead Reach — 5/side
8. Half-Kneeling Anti-Rotation Med Ball Shotput — 8/side, 4 lb
