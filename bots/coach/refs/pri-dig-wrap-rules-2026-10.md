## Warm-up selection

- Default reach order: **right reach on exhale first**; left reach only after L AF IR / IO-TA hold.
- 90/90 non-responders → PRIVY-281 ladder:
  1. R sidelying L adductor pull-back ± balloon
  2. All Four L posterior mediastinum
  3. Wall squat ± balloon
  4. Short-seated balloon
  5. Standing alternating respiratory reach
  6. Late: L AIC stance + R reach
- Patho PEC gate: can toe-touch / deep squat + bilateral ADT/PADT (+) → inhibit / posterior expansion first; don’t jump to classic L AIC facilitation.

## Soft press / OH / swim tertiary

- Soft press / OH only when rib IR + R apical without lat IR takeover (palm flat, elbow in-line, down-shrug).
- Prefer lat hang w/ balloon, squatting bar reach, standing serratus squat over early lat-dominant pulls.
- Swim / OH: lat inhibit + R LT/tricep + serratus; HG IR via **subscap not lat**.
- Right BC: L LT/serratus before R LT/tricep if apical still asymmetric.
