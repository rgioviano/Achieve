### NEW / under-used for Achieve gym prescriptions (this dig)
1. **PRIVY #281 (2022)** — Explicit ladder when athletes **do not respond to 90-90 hip lifts** (adductor pull-back + balloon; All Four L posterior mediastinum; wall squat + balloon / apical expansion; short-seated balloon variants; standing alternating respiratory reach; late Left AIC stance + R arm reach) + co-morbidity checklist (vision/occlusion/cervical/ankle/airway).
2. **PRIVY #335 (2023)** — Six gym-relevant reasons to **inhibit right lat** (UE swing without proximal hiking; open L posterior mediastinum fill; reduce distal gripping; allow traps/hamstrings/arms to move; restore ability to move ZOA “back”; avoid only being driven “forward”).
3. **PRIVY #369 (2024)** — **Thoracic inlet/outlet** conservative pathway with Achieve-safe NM names: Lat Hang w/ Balloon, Squatting Bar Reach, Standing Serratus Squat, Standing Lat Stretch → then R apical / R LT–tricep / **HG IR via subscapularis not lat/pec**; Type I scapula compression vs first-rib (L rib / R Superior T4 vs R subclavius) branches.
4. **Reach sequencing (Weidauer, PRI PDF)** — **Right reach on exhale first** to establish left ZOA / right apical path; **left reach later** to integrate L IO/TA while rotating trunk right and opening L posterior mediastinum. Directly affects warm-up balloon/reach choices and soft-press/OH progressions.
5. **Cantrell “Latissimus: The Secret Player”** — Practical silence cue: during L sidelying IO/TA + L adductor + R glute max, palm flat on abdomen, elbow in-line, shoulder down-shrugged — avoid wrist flex / elbow travel rearward (that recruits lat as translator). Wall squat / reach to elongate lat under diaphragmatic inhale.
6. **Swimmer Dyssynchrony Syndrome (Hruska 2006)** — Concise coach language: dyssynchrony = wrong muscle, wrong time, wrong position; acquired swimmer stack (forward head, rounded uneven shoulders, weak glutes, deep low back, hyperextended knees, elevated dry-land ribs); lat→IR takeover; scalene/SCM as primary breathers; synchrony depends on airflow + abs/hams/glutes/low traps/triceps/IR of arms-legs-ribs.
7. **Patho PEC floor screen (Pelvis Restoration, rev. Mar 2024 — already in extract but under-operationalized for Achieve wraps)** — `+` bilateral ADT **and** `+` PADT **and** ability to touch toes (or Functional Squat Level 4–5) ≈ Patho PEC → **inhibit / posterior expansion / abs+PPT+adductors first**; do **not** jump to classic Left AIC facilitation-heavy menus. Contrast: Left AIC often **cannot** touch toes with `+` ADT.
8. **Overhead / thrower / VB rib–scap chain** (Bartels volleyball series; baseball pitching series; Type II scapula I&I appendix) — Internally rotated right ribs under scapula → lost convex rib surface → serratus/low trap/subscap disadvantaged → **lat takes IR** → anterior GH stress. Restore rib/ZOA **before** isolated cuff. Sidelying left AF IR screen usable by S&C (Bartels).
9. **Posterior mediastinum language (Jen Platt, posturalrestoration.com)** — “Inhibition” of L posterior mediastinum = dial down overactivity **and** facilitate **airflow expansion** while holding left ZOA / IO-TA opposition (not anterior rib flare).
10. **Peer-reviewed (limited but real)** — Boyle et al. clinical application of R sidelying respiratory L adductor pull-back (IJSPT 2013); Boyle/Olinick/Lewis balloon 90/90 (NAJSPT 2010); Jackson/Purvis/Brown RCT of PRI non-manuals vs traditional posture work in collegiate males (IJSPT 2019: FA IR / abduction symmetry, left ADT, small pro-agility change; no VJ change); Petroll et al. 1990 J Appl Physiol on ZOA mechanics (not PRI-branded, supports ZOA construct).

## 2. Actionable Achieve prescription bullets

Grounded changes for warm-up / tertiary / soft-press & OH gates / non-manuals (gym-safe only):

1. **Warm-up ZOA block — reach order:** Default balloon / wall / squat reaches with **right arm reach on exhale first** (left ZOA + right apical). Add **left reach** only after left AF IR / IO-TA hold exists, when the goal is right trunk rotation + L posterior mediastinum fill (Weidauer; Right BC guidelines ZOA list).
2. **90-90 non-responders:** If 90-90 hip lift ± balloon does not change position cues, rotate to PRIVY-281 ladder before “more of the same”: R sidelying respiratory L adductor pull-back ± balloon → All Four L posterior mediastinum in L AF IR → PRI wall-supported squat ± balloon / R apical → short-seated balloon / wall short-seated L arm reach → standing alternating respiratory reach → late Left AIC stance + R arm reach. Flag referral/co-factors if still stuck (vision, occlusion, cervical, airway).
3. **Patho PEC gate before classic L AIC menus:** Athletes who **can** deep toe-touch / deep squat **and** show bilateral ADT(+)/PADT(+) → prioritize PEC/Patho PEC repositioning (wall-supported reach, 90-90 in passive FA IR + balloon, modified all-fours belly lift family, abs + posterior pelvic tilt + adductors / posterior expansion) **before** aggressive Left AIC facilitation (left glute med / ICA heavy progressions).
4. **Soft press / OH gate — lat silence before load:** Gate soft press / OH when athlete can show rib IR + R apical without lat IR takeover. Cue palm-flat abdomen / elbow in-line / down-shrug on repositioning drills; prefer squatting bar reach, standing serratus squat, lat hang w/ balloon / low trap over heavy lat-dominant pull-downs early (Cantrell; PRIVY-335/369; shoulder prescription).
5. **Swimmer / overhead tertiary:** Keep lat inhibition + R low trap/tricep + serratus + subscap IR theme (already in swimming + shoulder refs). Add Swimmer Dyssynchrony language for coaches: dry-land elevated ribs + lat-as-IR + accessory neck breathers = same problem. Prefer techniques from shoulder impingement list (tricep curls/extensions, bilateral/alternating tricep pull-downs, long-seated press-down w/ abs, paraspinal release w/ L abs, all-fours belly lift) and bicipital list (R trunk rotation + serratus punch themes) already extracted.
6. **Right BC sequence reminder for programming:** After ZOA abdominal NMs, if apical tests still show − R apical / + L apical, stabilize with **Left low trap + Left serratus program before Right low trap + Right tricep program** (Right BC Treatment Guidelines handout — already in refs; reaffirm for wrap authors).
7. **Thrower / VB dryland:** Do not lead with isolated cuff if R HG IR / horizontal abduction limited and R scapula looks protracted/winged (Type II). First restore L ZOA / rib ER on right via balloon repositioning + serratus; AF IR left-hip work for kinetic chain (Bartels / pitching series).
8. **Posterior mediastinum cueing:** Coach “fill the left mid-back with air while keeping left lower ribs down” = L posterior mediastinum **expansion** (= inhibition of shortened posterior wall), not “engage mid-back muscles” (Platt).
9. **Floor AF IR screen (optional S&C):** Sidelying left AF IR lift (Bartels) as yes/no for left inner thigh + left outer hip together without rolling — drives warm-up choice (left adductor / glute med family) when failed.
10. **Evidence posture for claims:** Prefer “PRI non-manual repositioning has case-series and small RCT support for alignment/ADT and limited agility change; efficacy literature remains sparse” — cite Boyle 2013 / Jackson 2019; do not oversell.

### NEW / under-used vs §1–5 of this file

1. **Moon & Kim 2026 (Applied Sciences)** — Single-session thoracopelvic protocol in 40 office workers with obvious dominant-arm scapular dyskinesis: **90/90 wall bridge + ball**, **dominant-side-lying adductor pull-back**, **non-dominant-side-lying glute max abduction** (breathing-cued; cites Boyle balloon framework). Immediate + 24 h: SDT grades shifted obvious→subtle both sides (dominant Kendall W 0.766); dominant passive GH IR ↑ / ER ↓; static LSST change only at position 2. Authors frame as **preliminary response patterns, not efficacy**. Gym read: pelvis/respiration block before isolated scapular work can move dynamic scapular grade and GH IR short-term — aligns with Achieve Patho PEC / OH “pelvis + ZOA first” gate; do not oversell as proof.

2. **Waldron et al. 2020 IJSPT case (missed in Oct 2 peer-reviewed set)** — D1 volleyball player, chronic R RC tendinopathy, **bilateral ADT(+)** / PEC presentation. Dropped traditional cuff program; used adapted **90/90 dead-bug diaphragmatic breathing**, **90/90 hip shift/hemi-bridge**, **R sidelying respiratory L adductor pull-back** (≥3×/wk). Pain 6→≤2; R shoulder IR +15°; HAdLT to 4 bilaterally; L EDT cleared by day 17. Gym read: reinforces **PEC/Patho PEC pelvic NM menu before cuff** for OH athletes with bilateral ADT(+).

3. **Houglum & Kelley (PRI clinical email) — Superior T4 / L low trap + L serratus program sequencing** — After R subclavius clears, **R arm reach (hand above head) precedes L arm reach** in the L LT/serratus block because R serratus drives COM left + R rib ER while L ZOA holds; later L reach engrains delay / L posterior rib fill without L rib ER. Gym read: **deepens** locked R-reach-first + R BC “L LT/serratus before R LT/tricep” gates; not a new reverse order.

4. **Skip George white paper — Right lower trap & assisted R trunk rotation** — R LT assists R trunk rotation **only after** L-oriented pelvis/sacrum + L ZOA + R apical airflow/ER; without that sequence, R LT/tricep work is patho-compensatory. Gym read: soft-press/OH / R LT–tricep tertiary stays gated behind L ZOA + R apical (already locked).

5. **Lori Thomsen — Hemi 90-90 frontal-plane inlet/outlet** — R sidelying hemi 90-90 FA IR: L femur adduction → L outlet abduct / L inlet adduct; L sidelying R glute max HE + FA abd: R femur abd → R outlet adduct / R inlet abduct. Guidelines p.104: sagittal proximal stability first (inhibit plantar flexors, hip flexors, back extensors, rib elevators; hamstrings/glutes plus serratus and oblique abs for rib IR / thorax retraction), then frontal unilateral/asymmetric **before** symmetrical work, then transverse / symmetrical presses or overhead only when brachial-chain measures and adduction-lift scores are near symmetrical and the earlier stages are primed.
3. **Inside step 4, left hamstring upright work before right (manual p.113):** Single-leg **left RDL** is early (sagittal; hold pelvis and rib cage back over the left heel). Single-leg **right RDL** is later, and only if the left ZOA is already shaped, lateralized, and stable in the frontal plane, so the COM does not dump into the right ab wall / right adductor. Right-side cues still pull the **left** pelvis up with the left adductor. Sheet names: “Left Early Stance with Right Arm Reach” (frontal; right hand toward left shin; exhale push through left heel; left hip stays high) vs “Right Stance with Left Ab Wall” (left hand toward right shin; left hip higher than right; left thorax over left hip; right knee stays out).
4. **Deadlift proficiency before squat; back-squat horizontal-abduction gate (manual p.111 and pp.114–116):** Bilateral symmetrical lifts keep the pelvic diaphragm back over the heels and the respiratory diaphragm back over the pelvic diaphragm. “Proficiency with dead lifting should precede squatting.” For a **back squat**, the lifter should have **>30°** shoulder horizontal abduction (sidelying or supine) to hold COM. Squat descent needs rib IR and thoracolumbar retraction; airflow cue given: inhale down, exhale up (optional exhale to set the thorax at the top). This 30° squat gate is **not** the Postural Respiration test cut-point (that extract still says horizontal abduction **<45°** is limited). Do not swap the numbers.
5. **Named sagittal-inhibition reaches stay in step 1, right arm on the exhale:** Short seated balloon: reach **right** arm while exhaling into the balloon so left lower ribs go down, in, and back; left abs > right; left hemipelvis stays elevated (sheet p.122). Squatting bar reach is labeled sagittal-plane inhibition (heels, ribs retracted, back-rib inhale; p.119), not an overhead load. Crab-walk reach-arounds (long-seated left AF IR, manual p.95): set left hip back / left AF IR first; step 2 **exhale, right-arm** reach; step 3 **inhale, left-arm** reach.
6. **Transverse-plane sheet stays behind L AF IR (manual p.94 / Respiratory Rib Integration sheet):** Sidelying intercostal inhibition in left AF IR — right arm arcs overhead while inhaling into the **right upper chest**. If the same drill is done on the right side, the left knee stays behind the right. Plane label in the sheet header is transverse, i.e. after the sagittal/frontal prime above.
7. **Wall-supported resisted reach (bilateral serratus punch, sagittal, p.137):** both arms reach forward/down on the exhale, ribs back into the wall. Optional alternation printed on the sheet: **left reach on the inhale, right reach on the exhale**, left back stays on the wall. Same-drill cue, not a new “left reach on exhale first” order.
8. **FLM hemi-chest chart is a gait cycle, not a floor menu (2022 PDF, hemi-chest pages).** Right-cycle primary muscles in stage order: early compression **lat** (deflate) → intercostals/QL → **IO/TA** → **serratus** → **triceps** (phase flips to inflate at late compression) → early decompression **lower trap**, task line “initial subscapula compression” → upper trap → scalenes/SCM. Upper-limb chart: lat and pec are forward-pull stages; triceps at pull-back; lower trap at early lift-up. Footnote: swing only counts when the opposite limb is on the ground. Use only as a reason not to load swing-side or late-decompression muscles before stance-side security and compression-phase abs/serratus. Do not turn the lat-first column into exercise one.
9. **FLM whiteboard, speed (page 3, read visually):** heading “FLM Dyskinesia” — up arrow speed, up arrow “Need to Cease,” then down peripheral sense, down echolocation, **up BC decompression**, down pendular UE (early UE “lift up” and “pull in”), down interlimb and intralimb patterns. Floor read: faster work is where BC decompression goes up and the early arm pattern drops off. Not a new repositioning sequence. Pages 1–2 are cortex/bipedalism notes, no exercises.
10. **Red / yellow / green (manual pp.102–103)** is durability, not a new chain order: red = cannot get ZOAs in three planes, high tone, “bumpers,” retest 2–4 weeks, PRC encouraged. That group is the one that should not be advanced to step-5 symmetrical loading. Overlaps the existing “don’t load on a locked pattern” idea; the category names are the new part.

### Locked hierarchy — contradictions?

**No reversal found. Do not rewrite the lock.**

- Patho PEC first (toe-touch/deep squat + bilateral ADT/PADT + → inhibit/posterior expansion before classic L AIC facilitation): **not stated** in the F&M programming pages read (pp.101–117) or the six sheets. Keep the lock for athletes who can toe-touch/deep squat with bilateral ADT/PADT +.
- R reach on exhale before L reach: **supported** by short-seated right-arm balloon reach, crab-walk steps 2 then 3, left RFESS (right arm), and the wall-reach alternation (right on exhale, left on inhale). Not reversed.
- L LT/serratus before R LT/tricep: **not restated** in the F&M pages sampled. Postural Respiration text is unchanged and still has that day-2 order. No contradiction.
- Rib IR + right apical, lat quiet, before soft press/OH; HG IR via subscap not lat: F&M p.104 puts symmetrical presses/overhead behind sagittal + frontal prime and near-symmetrical BC and adduction-lift scores.
