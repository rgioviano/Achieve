## 1. Findings

### A. New peer-reviewed / preprint
1. **Zafar A, Kumar PC, Palekar TJ, Rathi M, Epari V.** Efficacy of Myokinematics with Postural Restoration Techniques on Pain and Disability among Individuals with Chronic Mechanical Neck Pain: A Randomized Controlled Trial. *Int J Drug Deliv Technol.* 2026;16(5):110–118. DOI 10.25258/ijddt.16.5.11. PDF: https://impactfactor.org/PDF/IJDDT/16/IJDDT,Vol16,Issue5,Article11.pdf
   - 84 adults (mean 47 y) finished 6 weeks. Both groups got conventional PT; experimental group swapped part of it for 25 min of PRI Brachial Chain work (7 manual + 7 non-manual).
   - NPRS drop 4.21 vs 3.00; NDI improvement 16.12 vs 6.90 (paper reports large effect sizes).
   - Non-manual list in the paper (images 11–17): resisted diagonal shoulder flexion, GH ER with glute med, scapular retraction with trunk rotation, Seated Serratus Punch, standing lat/lower trap/QL stretch, sternal positional Swiss ball stretch, bilateral corner stretch, sidelying Swiss ball inhibition with contralateral apical expansion and balloon, sidelying apical expansion with balloon. Paper's names are its own shorthand, not always the exact PRI manual titles.
   - Caveats (My inference): journal is a drug-delivery title, not a rehab journal; manual and non-manual were bundled, so the non-manual effect can't be separated; adult clinical neck pain, not athletes. Treat as weak-to-moderate support that BC/apical-expansion work helps neck pain, not as a gym protocol.
2. **Schroth vs PRI exercise in adolescent idiopathic scoliosis - RCT protocol** (Research Square preprint, 2026-10-04). https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8758226/v1 - 90 adolescents, 12 weeks, Cobb angle primary outcome. Protocol only, no results. Watch for results in later digs.
3. **Older, not previously logged:** Richardson C. *Effects of a Postural Restoration Focused Intervention on Muscle Activation During Deadlifts.* ODU MS thesis, 2019. https://digitalcommons.odu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1043&context=hms_etds - 16 lifters; an acute PRI-inspired breathing routine between hex-bar sets changed no muscle activation (back extensors, glutes, hamstrings, obliques); only a small posterior shift of right-foot center of pressure at 50% 1RM. Author's conclusion: short-term non-manual PRI does not appear to change deadlift mechanics. Unpublished thesis, small n.

   - Forward head, neck that won't relax on reaches, limited horizontal abduction or neck rotation → Standing Serratus Squat (Anterior Neck Inhibition).
