# Sales and prospects

## Flow
1. Inquiry arrives (website lead form, Meta Instant Form, call, SMS, email).
2. **Tally** logs it in the Prospect Tracking sheet (2026 tab, newest at top). Tally is the only CRM. Daily 8:00am CT pass on WordPress Lead Form emails. (TEAM.md, CRM ownership 2026-09-16)
3. First contact fast (speed to contact target 60 seconds or less; Sales owns live contact, Marketing owns the form trigger). (Max 2026-09-08)
4. Book the evaluation (Ryne books; evenings). Penny prepares a prep card about 30 minutes before each eval.
5. Eval and close. Then follow-up if they did not sign.
6. After sign-up, **Eval Brief** drafts a Slack #clients coach brief from the Drive assessment (draft-first; Ryne approves). Gate: Prospect Tracking Sign-Up = Yes.

## Prospect Tracking stages (locked 2026-10-06)
New inquiry > Replied > Eval booked > Eval done > Offer > Signed / Lost / Hold. Tally owns stage and contact count, using evidence from Gmail, Quo, calendar, and the sheet only.

## Rules
- No prices on inbound. Rates are quoted at the eval.
- Lead nurture is the **3-7-30** value sequence, not chase. Sales owns email nurture; Marketing uses it only for paid nurture. (Max, Maya 2026-09-08)
- Do not ask to set up an eval 3 or 4 times in a row. (Max 2026-08-31)
- Cancel or drop-off replies: offer 1x/week first ("what most athletes do in-season"), not "sorry to hear that", not fees first.
- Varsity parents: open with the force-plate eval and a program written from their jumps.
- **Reactivate win-backs:** Penny finds people who stopped, labels the last thread Reactivate, sets a reach date; Sales drafts. Wait at least 2 months after a cancel; context wins (HS in-season waits until offseason); skip angry leavers. (Max 2026-09-09)
- Past-lead batches: Ryne approves final text and recipient list every time.
- Gmail filing: see `gmail-waiting-vs-follow-up`. Archived means done.

## Numbers
- Planning assumptions: about 90% show, about 87% close; about 20 booked evals per month needed for about 15 enrolls. Real 2026 show/close is being measured by marking every eval Eval done or No-show; review about mid-December 2026. (Lead plan 1a, #9)
