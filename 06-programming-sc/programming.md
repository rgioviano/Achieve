# Programming (S&C)

## Who writes
- **Coach** drafts programs (posts drafts in chat only). Ryne puts them in TrainHeroic and assigns. No bot writes or assigns in TrainHeroic unless Ryne says so. (Coach 2026-09-03)
- **Hawkin** bot supplies force-plate data (session-best CMJ/SJ, trends). **PRI** bot covers PRI patterns. **Darwin** brings research (Saturday dig).

## Before drafting
1. Asana Programs board notes for that person (goals, injuries, constraints).
2. Their last TrainHeroic block (Ryne opens or pastes it; do not scrape TrainHeroic).
3. Hawkin data first for athletes. Plates are the #1 input, but also weigh athlete-reported gaps and gym performance.

## House session (from Ryne's TrainHeroic programs)
- A Prep/SMR, B Breathing, C Movement Prep, D jump + med ball, E/F strength pairs, G core/PRI, H finisher. Pairs by letter; never write "superset".
- 4-week block, same exercises, progress reps/holds, Week 4 volume deload. Next block: keep at most 1 or 2 primary lifts, change nearly everything else.
- Default athlete report: 2 days/week, each day lower power + upper power + primary lower + primary upper, each with a short why that cites the plate data.
- Every exercise name comes from the Achieve progression sheet or house skills. Do not invent names.
- Overhead athletes: no true overhead press; end sessions with 2 core + 1 shoulder-health.
- Equipment: assume full Achieve gear (dipping belts exist). Never apply the club's thin-equipment limits to Achieve.
- Plain English in posts. No jargon pile-ups.

## Format for Ryne
- Apple Notes ready: day headers, lettered slots, exercise + sets/reps on one line, start load, short why in italics.
